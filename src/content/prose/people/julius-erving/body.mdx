---
title: Career
---

Julius Erving was the ABA's great star, its MVP three years in a row, before joining Philadelphia in 1976 when the ABA and NBA merged.

He spent eleven seasons with the 76ers, was NBA <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> in 1981 and won the 1983 title. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 1993.
