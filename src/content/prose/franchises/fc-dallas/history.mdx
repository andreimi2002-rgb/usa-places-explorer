---

### The Dallas Burn (1996–2004)

The club played its first match on April 14, 1996, and Jason Kreis scored its first goal; he was MVP in 1999. The Burn won the U.S. Open Cup in 1997 and played most of these years at the Cotton Bowl.

### FC Dallas (2005–today)

Renamed FC Dallas for 2005, the club moved that August into its own stadium in Frisco. The Colombian David Ferreira was MVP in 2010, the year Dallas reached the MLS Cup final and lost to Colorado after extra time. In 2016 it won the Supporters' Shield and the U.S. Open Cup.
