---
title: Career
---

Steve Kerr was a guard on the Chicago Bulls teams that won the 1996, 1997 and 1998 titles alongside Michael Jordan.

He became head coach of the Warriors in 2014 and won the title in his first season. Under him Golden State won four titles, in 2015, 2017, 2018 and 2022, and he is still the head coach in 2026–27.
