### Roots (1975–2010)

The first Portland Timbers played in the old North American Soccer League from 1975 to 1982. A lower-league Timbers club followed from 2001 to 2010.

### MLS (2011–today)

The MLS Timbers played their first match on March 19, 2011. Diego Valeri, signed from Lanús in 2013, became their star: MLS Cup MVP when Portland won the 2015 final against Columbus, and league MVP in 2017. Portland lost the 2018 final to Atlanta and the 2021 final to New York City FC on penalties, and won the MLS is Back tournament in 2020. The captain Diego Chará has been with the club since its first MLS season.
