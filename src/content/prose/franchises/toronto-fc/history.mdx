### The early years (2007–2014)

Toronto played its first home match at BMO Field on May 12, 2007, a 3–1 win over Chicago.

### The treble team (2015–2019)

In 2015 the club signed Giovinco, who won the MVP award in his first season, and Jozy Altidore, beside the captain Michael Bradley. Toronto lost the 2016 final to Seattle on penalties, then beat Seattle 2–0 in 2017 to complete the treble. It reached the CONCACAF Champions League final in 2018 and lost a third MLS Cup final to Seattle in 2019.

### Rebuilding (2020–today)

Alejandro Pozuelo was MVP in 2020. In 2022 the club signed the Italians Lorenzo Insigne and Federico Bernardeschi. Jonathan Osorio, a Torontonian, has been captain since 2024.
