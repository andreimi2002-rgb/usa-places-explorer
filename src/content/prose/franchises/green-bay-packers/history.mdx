### Lambeau's club (1919–1958)

Curly Lambeau and George Whitney Calhoun founded the club on August 11, 1919. Lambeau coached until 1949 and the team won several early NFL titles.

### Lombardi (1959–1967)

Vince Lombardi arrived in February 1959 and built a dynasty around the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Bart Starr: five NFL championships, including Super Bowls I and II.

### Favre and Rodgers (1992–today)

Brett Favre, acquired in 1992, won three MVP awards and Super Bowl XXXI. Aaron Rodgers, drafted in 2005, won Super Bowl XLV. Jordan Love has been the quarterback since Rodgers left, and the pass rusher Micah Parsons joined in 2025.
