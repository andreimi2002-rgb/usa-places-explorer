### Cleveland and Los Angeles (1936–1994)

Founded in Cleveland in 1936, the Rams won the 1945 NFL title and then moved to Los Angeles. They won again in 1951. The 1960s "Fearsome Foursome" defensive line included Deacon Jones and Merlin Olsen, and Eric Dickerson set the single-season rushing record in 1984.

### St. Louis (1995–2015)

In St. Louis, the "Greatest Show on Turf" offence of Kurt Warner and Marshall Faulk won Super Bowl XXXIV.

### Back in Los Angeles (2016–today)

Owner Stan Kroenke brought the team back in 2016 and hired Sean McVay in 2017. The Rams lost Super Bowl LIII, opened SoFi Stadium in 2020 and won Super Bowl LVI there over Cincinnati. In January 2026 they lost the NFC final 31–27 in Seattle; that spring they traded for Myles Garrett and Trent McDuffie, and Aaron Donald returned from retirement.
