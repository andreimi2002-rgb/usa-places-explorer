### The early years (1967–2005)

The Saints began in 1967 and moved into the Superdome in 1975. Archie Manning, father of Peyton and Eli, was their quarterback in the 1970s; the Danish kicker Morten Andersen and the linebacker Rickey Jackson starred in the 1980s.

### Brees and Payton (2006–2021)

Brees and Payton arrived in 2006. The Saints won Super Bowl XLIV over Indianapolis, with Brees as MVP, and remained contenders until Brees retired in 2021.

### Today (2022–today)

Gayle Benson, who also owns the Pelicans, has owned the club since 2018. Kellen Moore became coach in 2025, when the rookie quarterback Tyler Shough took over during a 6–11 season.
