### Expansion (1977–1988)

The Blue Jays began in 1977 at Exhibition Stadium. The <Term id="pitcher">pitcher</Term> Dave Stieb led their first good teams.

### The SkyDome champions (1989–1993)

The team moved into the SkyDome, with its retractable roof, in 1989. With Roberto Alomar and Joe Carter, it won the World Series in 1992 and 1993.

### Today (1994–today)

Roy Halladay, Carlos Delgado and José Bautista starred in later years. Vladimir Guerrero Jr., son of a Hall of Famer, leads today's team, which reached the 2025 World Series under John Schneider.
