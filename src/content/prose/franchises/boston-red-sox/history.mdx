### The early champions (1901–1919)

Founded in 1901 for the new American League, the club won the first World Series in 1903 and four more by 1918, with Cy Young and the young <Term id="pitcher">pitcher</Term> Babe Ruth. Fenway Park opened in 1912.

### The long wait (1920–2003)

After Ruth was sold to New York, the Red Sox lost four World Series, all in seven games, despite Ted Williams and Carl Yastrzemski.

### The curse broken (2004–today)

John Henry's group bought the club in 2002. With David Ortiz and Pedro Martínez, Boston won the 2004 World Series, and again in 2007, 2013 and 2018. Chad Tracy became manager in April 2026, after Alex Cora.
