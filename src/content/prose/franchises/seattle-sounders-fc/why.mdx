---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Sounders are one of the clubs that showed MLS could draw big crowds: they play in Seattle's NFL stadium, Lumen Field. Since joining in 2009 they have won two <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cups</Term>, in 2016 and 2019, and in 2022 became the first MLS club to win the modern CONCACAF Champions League.
