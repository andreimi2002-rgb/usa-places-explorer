---
title: Career
---

The Knicks drafted Willis Reed in 1964, and he was Rookie of the Year. He spent his whole career in New York and was its captain and center.

In 1970 he was league <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> and <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term> as the Knicks won their first title, and he was Finals MVP again in 1973. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 1982 and died on March 21, 2023.
