### Oakland and Al Davis (1960–1981)

Founded in 1960 for the new American Football League, the Raiders hired Al Davis as coach in 1963; he later became the owner. They won the 1967 AFL title, and under John Madden, coach from 1969 to 1978, Super Bowl XI. Tom Flores's team won Super Bowl XV.

### Los Angeles (1982–1994)

In Los Angeles the Raiders won Super Bowl XVIII, 38–9 over Washington, with Marcus Allen as MVP.

### Oakland again (1995–2019)

Back in Oakland, they reached Super Bowl XXXVII and lost to Tampa Bay. Al Davis died in 2011; his son Mark Davis took over.

### Las Vegas (2020–today)

The Raiders moved to Las Vegas in 2020. Tom Brady bought a minority stake in 2024. After a 3–14 season in 2025 they hired Klint Kubiak, who had just won Super Bowl LX as Seattle's offensive coordinator, and drafted the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Fernando Mendoza first overall.
