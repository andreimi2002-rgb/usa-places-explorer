### ABA years (1967–1976)

The club began as the New Jersey Americans in 1967 and became the New York Nets a year later, playing on Long Island. With Julius Erving it won the ABA title in 1974 and 1976, the last one before the merger.

### New Jersey (1977–2012)

The Nets moved to New Jersey in 1977. Dražen Petrović joined in 1991 and died in 1993. With Jason Kidd at point guard, they reached the NBA Finals in 2002 and 2003, losing to the Lakers and then San Antonio. Vince Carter starred for them from 2005 to 2009.

### Brooklyn (2012–today)

On November 3, 2012, the Nets played their first game as the Brooklyn Nets at the new Barclays Center. Kevin Durant and Kyrie Irving played there in the early 2020s, and Joe Tsai took full ownership in 2019. Coached by Jordi Fernández, the 2026–27 Nets pair Julius Randle and Michael Porter Jr. with young players such as Egor Dëmin.
