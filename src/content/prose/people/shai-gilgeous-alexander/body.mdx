title: Career
---

Charlotte picked Shai Gilgeous-Alexander 11th in the 2018 <Term id="nba-draft">draft</Term>, and he spent his rookie season with the LA Clippers before joining Oklahoma City in 2019.

In 2024–25 he was league <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term>, scoring champion and <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>, as the Thunder beat Indiana in Game 7 for the title. He was named MVP again in 2025–26.
