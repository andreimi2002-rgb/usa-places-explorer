---
title: Career
---

Born Ferdinand Lewis Alcindor Jr., Kareem Abdul-Jabbar was drafted first overall by Milwaukee in 1969 and won a title there. In 1975 Milwaukee <Term id="trade">traded</Term> him to the Lakers for four players.

In fourteen seasons in Los Angeles he won five more titles and became the anchor of the Showtime teams. He was league <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> six times, and he finished his career with 38,387 points. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 1995.
