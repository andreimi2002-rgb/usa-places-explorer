---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Earthquakes, first called the San Jose Clash, won MLS's very first match in 1996 and the <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cup</Term> in 2001 and 2003 with a young Landon Donovan. After 2005 the team moved to Houston, but San Jose kept the name, records and trophies and came back to MLS in 2008. Chris Wondolowski, a San Jose player for most of his career, retired as the league's all-time top scorer.
