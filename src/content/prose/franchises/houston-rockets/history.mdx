---

### San Diego (1967–1971)

The Rockets joined the NBA as an expansion team in San Diego in 1967–68 and moved to Houston in 1971.

### Moses Malone (1976–1982)

Moses Malone arrived in 1976 and was league <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> twice in Houston.

### Hakeem and two titles (1984–2001)

Houston drafted Hakeem Olajuwon first overall in 1984. Rudy Tomjanovich, a former Rockets player, became coach in 1992. In 1994 the Rockets beat the Knicks in seven games for their first title, with Olajuwon as MVP and Finals MVP. In 1995 they added Clyde Drexler and swept Orlando for a second.

### Yao, McGrady and Harden (2002–2021)

Yao Ming was the first pick of the 2002 draft and spent his whole career in Houston. Tracy McGrady joined in 2004, and James Harden starred from 2012 to 2021. In 2017 Tilman Fertitta bought the club.

### Durant and the young core (2025–today)

Kevin Durant joined in 2025. Coached by Ime Udoka, the 2026–27 Rockets pair him with Alperen Şengün, Amen Thompson and Fred VanVleet.
