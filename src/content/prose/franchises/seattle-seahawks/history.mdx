### Expansion and the AFC (1976–2001)

Founded in 1974, the Seahawks began play in 1976 at the Kingdome and spent 1977 to 2001 in the AFC. The wide receiver Steve Largent and the tackle Walter Jones became Hall of Famers.

### Carroll's champions (2002–2023)

Back in the NFC from 2002, and in today's stadium, Seattle lost Super Bowl XL. Pete Carroll, coach from 2010 to 2023, built the team of Russell Wilson, Marshawn Lynch and Richard Sherman that won Super Bowl XLVIII and lost XLIX.
