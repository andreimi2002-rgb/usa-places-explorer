---
title: For European fans
---

**Watching from Europe.** Orlando is on Eastern Time, so a 7:00 pm tip-off is midnight in London and 2:00 am in Bucharest for most of the season. Weekend afternoon games are the easiest to watch live. The clock further down this page converts any time for you.

**The Magic in Europe.** In January 2026 Orlando played two regular-season games in Europe against the Memphis Grizzlies: a 118–111 win in Berlin on January 15, the first NBA regular-season game ever played in Germany, and a 126–109 loss in London three days later.

**Europeans on the team.** Franz Wagner, one of the faces of the current team, was born in Berlin and was the eighth pick of the 2021 draft. The 2026-27 roster also includes the German-born Tristan da Silva, the French-born Noah Penda, the Georgian-born Goga Bitadze and Nikola Vučević, who was born in Switzerland and played for Montenegro's national team until 2025. Earlier, the Turkish forward Hedo Türkoğlu was named the league's Most Improved Player in Orlando in 2008 and was a key part of the 2009 Finals team.

**Visiting.** The NBA regular season runs from October to April, which in Orlando falls mostly in the drier, milder half of the year; the wet season, with near-daily summer storms, usually runs from late May to mid-October.
