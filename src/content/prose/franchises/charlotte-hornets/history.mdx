title: History by eras
---

### The original Hornets (1988–2002)

The Hornets joined the NBA in 1988. Muggsy Bogues and Dell Curry were with them from the start, and Larry Johnson and Alonzo Mourning arrived in 1991 and 1992. In 2002 the club moved to New Orleans.

### The Bobcats (2004–2014)

An expansion team named the Charlotte Bobcats began play in 2004 and moved into today's Spectrum Center in 2005. Michael Jordan became majority owner in 2010, and Kemba Walker was the team's star.

### The Hornets again (2014–today)

The club became the Hornets again for the 2014–15 season. In 2023 Rick Schnall and Gabe Plotkin bought the majority stake from Jordan. In 2026 the Hornets traded LaMelo Ball to Minnesota; coached by Charles Lee, they now build around Brandon Miller and Kon Knueppel.
