---
title: Career in Orlando
---

Nick Anderson was the first player Orlando ever chose in the <Term id="nba-draft">draft</Term>, taken 11th overall in 1989 for the club's debut season. A shooting guard, he played the franchise's first ten seasons, from the expansion years through the 1995 Finals run, and was one of the first two people inducted into the Magic's Hall of Fame in 2014.
