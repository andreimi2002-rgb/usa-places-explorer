---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Pelicans' franchise began in 2002, when the Hornets moved from Charlotte to New Orleans. In 2013 the team took the name Pelicans, and in 2014 Charlotte took back the Hornets name and the history of its original team, so the Pelicans' records start in 2002.

Its stars have been Chris Paul, Anthony Davis and, today, Zion Williamson. In 2026 the club hired Jamahl Mosley, who had coached Orlando from 2021 to 2026.
