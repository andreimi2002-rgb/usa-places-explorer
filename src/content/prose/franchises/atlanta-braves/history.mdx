### Boston (1871–1952)

The club began in 1871 and played in Boston under several names, including the Beaneaters and the Braves. The "Miracle Braves" won the 1914 World Series.

### Milwaukee (1953–1965)

In Milwaukee, with Hank Aaron, Eddie Mathews and the <Term id="pitcher">pitcher</Term> Warren Spahn, the Braves won the 1957 World Series.

### Atlanta (1966–today)

In Atlanta, Aaron hit his 715th home run on April 8, 1974. Under Bobby Cox the Braves won 14 division titles in a row and the 1995 World Series, with the pitchers Greg Maddux, Tom Glavine and John Smoltz and Chipper Jones. They won again in 2021 and moved into Truist Park in 2017. Walt Weiss became manager after the 2025 season, and the Braves won the NL East in 2026.
