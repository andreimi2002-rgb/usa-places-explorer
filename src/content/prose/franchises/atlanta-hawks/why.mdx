---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Hawks have lived in four places: they began in 1949 as the Tri-Cities Blackhawks, moved to Milwaukee in 1951, to St. Louis in 1955 and to Atlanta in 1968. Their only title came in St. Louis in 1958, when Bob Pettit, the NBA's first <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term>, scored 50 points in the deciding game against Boston.

In Atlanta their best-known stars have been Dominique Wilkins and, more recently, Trae Young, traded to Washington in 2026.
