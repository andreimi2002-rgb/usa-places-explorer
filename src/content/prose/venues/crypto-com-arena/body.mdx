---
title: About the arena
---

Crypto.com Arena opened in downtown Los Angeles on October 17, 1999, with a concert by Bruce Springsteen and the E Street Band. It was called Staples Center until December 25, 2021, when it took the name of the cryptocurrency exchange.

AEG owns and runs the building, which is home to three professional teams: the NBA's Lakers, the NHL's Kings and the WNBA's Sparks. The Lakers have won six titles while playing here, from 2000 to 2020.
