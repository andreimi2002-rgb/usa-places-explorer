---
title: About the city
---

Los Angeles has had the Lakers since 1960, when they arrived from Minneapolis, and the Clippers since 1984. The Rams played here from 1946 to 1994 and came back in 2016; the Chargers, who spent their first season here in 1960, returned from San Diego in 2017. In soccer, the LA Galaxy, a founding MLS club, play in Carson, and Los Angeles FC joined in 2018. Los Angeles runs on Pacific Time, eight hours behind London and ten behind Bucharest for most of the year.
