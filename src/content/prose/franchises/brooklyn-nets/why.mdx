---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Nets began in the ABA in 1967 and were its last champions: as the New York Nets, with Julius Erving, they won the ABA title in 1974 and 1976. They joined the NBA in 1976, spent 35 seasons in New Jersey, and moved to Brooklyn in 2012.

For Europeans they also hold a special place: Dražen Petrović, the Croatian guard, starred for New Jersey before his death in 1993.
