---
title: Career
---

Golden State drafted Stephen Curry seventh overall in 2009, and he has played his whole career with the Warriors. His long-range shooting became the signature of the Warriors' game.

He won titles in 2015, 2017, 2018 and 2022, and was <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term> in 2022. He was league <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> in 2015 and 2016, the second time as the first unanimous winner in history, after the Warriors' 73-win season.
