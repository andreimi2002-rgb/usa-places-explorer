### The long years (1883–1949)

The Phillies began in 1883. The <Term id="pitcher">pitcher</Term> Grover Cleveland Alexander starred in the 1910s, and they reached the World Series in 1915.

### Whiz Kids and Schmidt (1950–1993)

The young "Whiz Kids", with Robin Roberts and Richie Ashburn, reached the 1950 World Series. Mike Schmidt and Steve Carlton won the club's first title in 1980.

### Citizens Bank Park (2004–today)

With Jimmy Rollins, Chase Utley and Ryan Howard, the Phillies won the 2008 World Series. Bryce Harper signed in 2019; the team reached the World Series in 2022 and won the NL East in 2024 and 2025. Don Mattingly replaced Rob Thomson as manager in April 2026.
