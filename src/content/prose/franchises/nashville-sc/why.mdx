---
title: Why this team matters
---

Nashville SC joined MLS in 2020 and quickly became a contender. Its stadium, Geodis Park, opened in 2022 with 30,000 seats, the largest built for soccer in the United States and Canada. The German-born Hany Mukhtar was MVP in 2022, the club won the U.S. Open Cup in 2025 and the <Term id="supporters-shield">Supporters' Shield</Term> in 2026.
