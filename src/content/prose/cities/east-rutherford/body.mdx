---
title: About the city
---

East Rutherford, New Jersey, across the river from Manhattan, is home to MetLife Stadium, opened in 2010 for the NFL's Giants and Jets. It was also home to the Nets for much of their New Jersey era, before the 2012 move to Brooklyn. East Rutherford runs on Eastern Time, five hours behind London and seven behind Bucharest for most of the year.
