---
title: Sign-and-trade
---

A sign-and-trade starts with a <Term id="free-agency">free agent</Term>. Instead of the player simply joining a new team, his old team signs him to a new contract and immediately <Term id="trade">trades</Term> him, so it gets players or picks in return rather than losing him for nothing.

Miami used sign-and-trade deals in 2010 to bring in LeBron James and Chris Bosh.
