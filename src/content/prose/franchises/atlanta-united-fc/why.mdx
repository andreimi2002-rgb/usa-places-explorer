---
title: Why this team matters
---

Atlanta United changed what Americans thought an MLS expansion club could be. It joined in 2017, played in a new 42,500-seat setting of an NFL stadium, and in 2018 averaged 53,002 fans a game, the first MLS club above 50,000. That same year it won the <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cup</Term> in front of 73,019 people.
