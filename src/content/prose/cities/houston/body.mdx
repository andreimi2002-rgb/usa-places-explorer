---
title: About the city
---

Houston has had the Rockets since 1971, when the team arrived from San Diego, the Texans since 2002 and the Dynamo since 2006; the Dynamo won the MLS Cup in their first two seasons. The NFL's Oilers played here from 1960 until 1996, before moving to Tennessee. Houston runs on Central Time, six hours behind London and eight behind Bucharest for most of the year.
