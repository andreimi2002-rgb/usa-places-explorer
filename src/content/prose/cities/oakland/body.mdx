---
title: About the city
---

Oakland, across the bay from San Francisco, had the Raiders from 1960 to 1981 and again from 1995 to 2019, when they moved to Las Vegas, and the Warriors from 1971 to 2019, when they moved back to San Francisco. The Warriors won four NBA titles while based here. Oakland runs on Pacific Time, eight hours behind London and ten behind Bucharest for most of the year.
