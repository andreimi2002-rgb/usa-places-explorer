---
title: History by eras
---

### Ruth and Gehrig (1903–1945)

The club began in 1903 as the New York Highlanders and became the Yankees in 1913. Babe Ruth arrived in 1920 and the first Yankee Stadium opened in 1923, "the House that Ruth Built". With Lou Gehrig and then Joe DiMaggio, the Yankees won their first titles.

### DiMaggio, Mantle and Berra (1946–1964)

The Yankees won the World Series five years in a row from 1949 to 1953, with Mickey Mantle and the catcher Yogi Berra among the stars.

### Steinbrenner and Jeter (1973–2009)

George Steinbrenner bought the club in 1973. Derek Jeter and the closer Mariano Rivera won five titles between 1996 and 2009, the last in the first season of today's Yankee Stadium.

### Judge (2016–today)

Aaron Judge arrived in 2016 and won three MVP awards. Under Aaron Boone, manager since 2018, the Yankees reached the 2024 World Series and lost it to the Dodgers.
