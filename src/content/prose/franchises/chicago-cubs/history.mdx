### The early club (1876–1945)

Founded as the Chicago White Stockings, the team was called the Cubs from 1903 and won the World Series in 1907 and 1908. It moved into Wrigley Field in 1916.

### The wait (1946–2015)

The Cubs did not reach another World Series after 1945. Ernie Banks, "Mr. Cub", Ron Santo, Billy Williams, Ferguson Jenkins and Ryne Sandberg starred in teams that rarely won. The Ricketts family bought the club in 2009.

### 2016 and today (2016–today)

In 2016 the Cubs beat Cleveland in a seventh game that went to extra <Term id="inning">innings</Term> and won their first title since 1908. Craig Counsell has been manager since 2024; the Cubs went 89–73 in 2026.
