---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Browns were one of the great early teams: named after their first coach, Paul Brown, they won four AAFC titles and then four NFL championships, the first in their first NFL season, 1950. Jim Brown, three times <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term>, played here. In 1996 the team's players left for Baltimore, but Cleveland kept the name and history, and the Browns returned in 1999. They are one of four NFL teams never to have reached a <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowl</Term>.
