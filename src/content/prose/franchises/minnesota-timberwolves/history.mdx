---

### Beginnings (1989–1995)

Minnesota and Orlando joined the NBA together for the 1989–90 season, taking the league from 25 to 27 teams. The Timberwolves played their first season at the Metrodome and moved into Target Center in 1990.

### Garnett (1995–2007)

Minnesota drafted Kevin Garnett fifth overall in 1995, straight out of high school. He was MVP in 2004.

### Love, Towns and Garnett's return (2008–2020)

Kevin Love and later Karl-Anthony Towns led the team, and Garnett came back for his final seasons in 2015 and 2016.

### Edwards (2020–today)

Anthony Edwards arrived in 2020. Coached by Chris Finch, the 2026–27 Wolves pair him with LaMelo Ball, Rudy Gobert and Jaden McDaniels. The club will retire Garnett's No. 21 during the season.
