---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Warriors are one of the NBA's original clubs and its very first champion: as the Philadelphia Warriors they won the first BAA title in 1947. They have since moved across the country, played under three names, and won seven titles in all.

For today's fans, they are Stephen Curry's team. Drafted in 2009, Curry led the Warriors to four titles between 2015 and 2022, with his long-range shooting at the center of their game. In 2015–16 the team won 73 regular-season games, the most in NBA history.
