---
title: About the arena
---

The Magic's first home opened in January 1989 as the Orlando Arena, months before the team played its first game. It is where Orlando grew up as an NBA city: the expansion years, the O'Neal and Hardaway teams and the 1995 Finals all happened here.

After the team moved to the new downtown arena in 2010, the old building stood empty and was imploded on March 25, 2012.
