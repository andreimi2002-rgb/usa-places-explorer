### Arriving home

Tracy McGrady was born in Bartow, Florida, and went straight from high school to the NBA: Toronto drafted him ninth overall in 1997. After three seasons with the Raptors he joined Orlando in 2000, the same summer the club signed Grant Hill.

### The scorer (2000–2004)

Hill was soon lost to injury, and the team became McGrady's. In his first Orlando season he was named the league's Most Improved Player, and he then led the whole league in scoring twice in a row, with 32.1 points per game in 2002-03 and 28.0 in 2003-04. He was one of the most feared scorers in the league, but Orlando lost in the first round of the <Term id="playoffs">playoffs</Term> three years running, and in 2003-04 the team fell to 21–61.

In 2004 he was traded to Houston, where he played beside Yao Ming.

### Legacy

McGrady was a seven-time <Term id="all-star">All-Star</Term> and a seven-time <Term id="all-nba">All-NBA</Term> pick. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2017 and the Magic's Hall of Fame in 2018.
