### Founders and champions (1996–2005)

The San Jose Clash beat D.C. United 1–0 on April 6, 1996, in the league's first ever match. Renamed the Earthquakes in 1999, the club won the MLS Cup in 2001 and 2003 with Donovan and Dwayne De Rosario, and the <Term id="supporters-shield">Supporters' Shield</Term> in 2005.

### Departure and return (2006–today)

In 2006 the players and staff moved to Houston and became the Dynamo. A new Earthquakes club, owned by Lewis Wolff and John Fisher, returned in 2008 with the old history. It won the Supporters' Shield again in 2012, when Wondolowski was MVP, and opened PayPal Park in 2015. On May 18, 2019, Wondolowski became MLS's all-time top scorer. Bruce Arena, coach of D.C. United's first champions, has been in charge since November 2024, and in 2026 the club signed Timo Werner.
