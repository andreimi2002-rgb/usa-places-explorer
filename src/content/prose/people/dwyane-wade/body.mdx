---
title: Career
---

Miami drafted Dwyane Wade fifth overall in 2003, and three years later he led the Heat to their first title. In the 2006 Finals against Dallas, Miami lost the first two games and won the next four, and Wade was named <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>.

He won two more titles, in 2012 and 2013, alongside LeBron James and Chris Bosh. Apart from a season and a half in Chicago and Cleveland, he spent his whole career in Miami, retiring in 2019. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2023.
