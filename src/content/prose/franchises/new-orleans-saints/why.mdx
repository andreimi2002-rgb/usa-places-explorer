---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Saints are tied to their city like few teams. In 2005 Hurricane Katrina flooded New Orleans and damaged the Superdome, which had sheltered thousands of people. The team returned in 2006 with a new <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term>, Drew Brees, and a new coach, Sean Payton, and four years later won <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowl</Term> XLIV, a symbol of the city's recovery.
