title: Why this team matters
---

Washington's team was founded in Boston in 1932 and has played in the capital since 1937. It won NFL championships in 1937 and 1942 and three <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowls</Term> under the coach Joe Gibbs. In 2020 it dropped its old name, the Redskins, and since 2022 it has been the Commanders. Jayden Daniels, its <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term>, was the 2024 Offensive Rookie of the Year.
