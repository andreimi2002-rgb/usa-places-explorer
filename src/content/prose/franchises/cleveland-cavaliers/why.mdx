---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Cavaliers' story is tied to LeBron James. Cleveland drafted him first overall in 2003; he left for Miami in 2010, came back in 2014, and in 2016 led the Cavaliers to their only title.

In the 2016 Finals Cleveland trailed Golden State 3–1 in the Finals and became the first team ever to come back from that position.
