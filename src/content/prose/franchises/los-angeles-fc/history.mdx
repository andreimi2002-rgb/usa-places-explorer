### A fast start (2018–2021)

LAFC began play in 2018 in a new stadium near downtown. Carlos Vela, its first <Term id="designated-player">Designated Player</Term>, won the Golden Boot and the MVP award in 2019 as the team won the Supporters' Shield.

### Champions (2022–today)

In 2022 LAFC won a second Shield and its first MLS Cup, beating the Philadelphia Union on penalties after Gareth Bale's late equaliser. It lost the 2023 final to Columbus, won the U.S. Open Cup in 2024 and, in August 2025, signed Son Heung-min from Tottenham for an MLS-record fee.
