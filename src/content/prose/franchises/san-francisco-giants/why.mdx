---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Giants were one of the great clubs of New York, with Christy Mathewson, Mel Ott and Willie Mays, before moving to San Francisco in 1958. They have won eight <Term id="world-series">World Series</Term>, five in New York and three in San Francisco, in 2010, 2012 and 2014. Barry Bonds, the career leader in <Term id="home-run">home runs</Term>, played here.
