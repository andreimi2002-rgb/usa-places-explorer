### The early titles (1882–1945)

Founded in 1882 as the St. Louis Brown Stockings, the club became the Cardinals in 1900. It won its first World Series in 1926 with Rogers Hornsby and five more by 1946, several with Stan Musial.

### Gibson, Brock and Ozzie (1946–1995)

Bob Gibson and Lou Brock led the 1964 and 1967 champions, and the shortstop Ozzie Smith the 1982 team.

### Pujols and Molina (1996–today)

William DeWitt Jr.'s group bought the club in 1996. With Albert Pujols and the catcher Yadier Molina, the Cardinals won the World Series in 2006, in the first season of today's Busch Stadium, and in 2011. In 2026 Jordan Walker became the first Cardinal to win the Home Run Derby.
