### A fast start (1968–1975)

The NBA awarded Milwaukee a franchise on January 22, 1968, and the Bucks played their first game that October. In 1969 they drafted Lew Alcindor, later Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, and added Oscar Robertson. In 1970–71 they went 66–16 and won the title.

### The 1980s (1979–1989)

Sidney Moncrief, drafted in 1979, was twice Defensive Player of the Year.

### Ray Allen, Glenn Robinson and Michael Redd (1994–2011)

Glenn Robinson and Ray Allen led the Bucks around 2000, and Michael Redd after them.

### Giannis (2013–2026)

Milwaukee drafted Giannis Antetokounmpo 15th overall in 2013. He was MVP in 2019 and 2020, and in 2021, in the Bucks' third season at Fiserv Forum, he led them to their first title in 50 years and was named <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>. In 2026 the Bucks traded him to Miami.
