---
title: Career
---

The Milwaukee Hawks drafted Bob Pettit second in 1954, and he played all eleven seasons of his career with the franchise. In 1956 he was the NBA's first <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term>, and in 1958 he scored 50 points in the clinching game as the St. Louis Hawks beat Boston for the title. He won a second MVP in 1959, entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 1971 and died on September 24, 2026.
