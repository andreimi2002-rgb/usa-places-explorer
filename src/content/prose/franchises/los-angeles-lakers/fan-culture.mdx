---
title: City and fan culture
---

The Lakers share Los Angeles with a second NBA team, the Clippers, which play in their own arena, the Intuit Dome. The Lakers' history in the city goes back to 1960, and their earlier years belong to Minneapolis, which is why the franchise's first five titles are listed under the Minneapolis Lakers.

Los Angeles runs on Pacific Time: eight hours behind London and ten behind Bucharest for most of the year. A 7:30 PM game in Los Angeles starts at 3:30 AM in London.
