---
title: Why this team matters
---

The LA Galaxy are MLS's most successful club, with a record six <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cups</Term>, and its most famous. One of the league's ten founding clubs, the Galaxy brought David Beckham to America in 2007, a signing that changed the league, and later Robbie Keane, Steven Gerrard and Zlatan Ibrahimović.
