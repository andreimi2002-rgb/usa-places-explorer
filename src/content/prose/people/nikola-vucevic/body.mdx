### Arriving in the Howard trade

Nikola Vučević was born in Morges, Switzerland, and played college basketball at USC. Philadelphia drafted him 16th in 2011, and a year later he was one of the six players Orlando received in the four-team trade that sent Dwight Howard to the Lakers.

### Nine seasons (2012–2021)

A skilled center who scores, passes and shoots from outside, he became the steady centre of the rebuilding Magic and a two-time <Term id="all-star">All-Star</Term>, in 2019 and 2021. In March 2021 Orlando traded him to Chicago, where he played five seasons. He also played for Montenegro's national team until 2025.

### Back in Orlando

In 2026 he returned to the Magic as a veteran presence beside the young core. He is second in club history in rebounds (6,402) and third in points (10,445) and blocks (555).
