---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Lions won four NFL championships in the 1930s and 1950s, the last in 1957, and have not won one since; they are one of four teams never to have reached a <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowl</Term>. Their best-known tradition is the Thanksgiving Day home game, which goes back to 1934. Under Dan Campbell, coach since 2021, they have become one of the NFC's strongest teams.
