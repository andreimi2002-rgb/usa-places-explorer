### The Boston Patriots (1960–1970)

The club was founded in 1959 as the Boston Patriots, one of the original AFL teams.

### New England (1971–1999)

In 1971 it moved to Foxborough and became the New England Patriots. It lost Super Bowls XX and XXXI. Robert Kraft bought the club in 1994.

### Brady and Belichick (2000–2019)

Belichick was hired in 2000 and Brady drafted the same year. Together they won Super Bowls XXXVI, XXXVIII, XXXIX, XLIX, LI and LIII. Brady left in March 2020; Belichick left after the 2023 season.

### Today (2024–today)

Drake Maye was drafted in 2024. Under Mike Vrabel, coach from 2025, the team went 14–3, won its division and reached Super Bowl LX.
