---
title: About the arena
---

The Kaseya Center is the Miami Heat's home. It opened in 1999, and the Heat played their first game there on January 2, 2000, beating the Orlando Magic 111–103 in front of 19,600 fans. The arena is owned by Miami-Dade County.

The building is a good lesson in how American arenas are named: companies buy the right to put their name on it. It was American Airlines Arena from 1999 to 2021, then FTX Arena until the cryptocurrency exchange collapsed, then Miami-Dade Arena for a few months in 2023, and Kaseya Center since April 2023.
