---
title: About the city
---

Chicago has had the Bears since 1921, a year after they were founded in Decatur; the Bulls since 1966; and the Fire since 1998, when the club won the MLS Cup in its first season. The NFL's Cardinals, today in Arizona, also began here and stayed until 1959. The Bears and, again since 2020, the Fire play at Soldier Field on the lakefront. Chicago runs on Central Time, six hours behind London and eight behind Bucharest for most of the year.
