---
title: About the city
---

Dallas has had the Cowboys since 1960 and the Mavericks since 1980; the Mavericks won the 2011 title. The Cowboys have played in Arlington, between Dallas and Fort Worth, since 2009. FC Dallas began in 1996 as the Dallas Burn and has played in Frisco, north of the city, since 2005. Dallas runs on Central Time, six hours behind London and eight behind Bucharest for most of the year.
