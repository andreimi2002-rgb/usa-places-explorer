### The AFL years (1960–1969)

Lamar Hunt founded the club in 1959 for the new American Football League. As the Dallas Texans it won the 1962 AFL title, then moved to Kansas City in 1963 and became the Chiefs. Coached by Hank Stram, it lost Super Bowl I to Green Bay and won Super Bowl IV over Minnesota, with Len Dawson as MVP.

### The NFL (1970–2012)

The Chiefs joined the NFL in the 1970 merger and moved into Arrowhead Stadium in 1972. Stars such as Derrick Thomas and Tony Gonzalez followed, but the team went fifty years without another Super Bowl.

### Reid and Mahomes (2013–today)

Andy Reid became coach in 2013 and Mahomes started at quarterback from 2018. Kansas City won Super Bowls LIV, LVII and LVIII and also reached LV and LIX.
