---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Jazz were founded in New Orleans in 1974 and kept their name when they moved to Salt Lake City in 1979.

Their great era came in the 1990s, when John Stockton and Karl Malone, coached by Jerry Sloan, reached the NBA Finals in 1997 and 1998, losing both times to Michael Jordan's Bulls. The Jazz are still waiting for their first title.
