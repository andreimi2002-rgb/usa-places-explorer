---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Rays are one of baseball's smallest-budget teams and one of its most inventive, known for analytics and clever trades. Founded as the Devil Rays in 1998, they have reached three <Term id="world-series">World Series</Term>, in 2008, 2020 and 2026, without winning one yet. After a hurricane damaged their dome in 2024, they spent a season in Tampa before returning home in 2026.
