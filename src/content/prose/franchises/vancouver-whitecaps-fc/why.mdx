---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Whitecaps carry a name that goes back to 1974 and the old NASL, where they won the 1979 Soccer Bowl. The MLS club joined in 2011 and had its best season in 2025: a record 63 points, the CONCACAF Champions Cup final and the <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cup</Term> final, lost 3–1 to Inter Miami, with Thomas Müller in the team.
