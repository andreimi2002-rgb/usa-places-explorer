### The city

Orlando is the main city of inland Central Florida, with just over 300,000 people at the 2020 census and a much larger metropolitan area around it. Most visitors know it for its theme parks, which sit south-west of the centre, but the city itself has a compact downtown, built around Lake Eola park, and that is where the Magic's arena stands.

### Sports in Orlando

Orlando is a young major-league city. The Magic arrived in 1989 and were its only top-level team for a quarter of a century. Since then the city has added Orlando City SC, which joined Major League Soccer in 2015, and the Orlando Pride, who joined the National Women's Soccer League in 2016; both play at Inter&Co Stadium, west of downtown.

The University of Central Florida competes as the UCF Knights and joined the Big 12, one of the major college conferences, in 2023. The city-owned Camping World Stadium hosts college football's Citrus Bowl, and the Kia Center is also home to the Orlando Solar Bears, a minor-league ice hockey team. These clubs will get their own pages as the encyclopedia adds more sports.

### Visiting

The main gateway is Orlando International Airport (MCO), south-east of downtown. Inside the city, SunRail, a commuter train, runs north–south through downtown and stops a few minutes from the arena. The climate is warm all year: the wet season, with near-daily afternoon thunderstorms, usually runs from late May to mid-October, while the basketball season falls mostly in the drier, milder months.
