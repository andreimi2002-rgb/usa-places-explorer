---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Dynamo were born when MLS moved the San Jose Earthquakes' players and staff to Houston at the end of 2005, while San Jose kept its name and history. The new club won the <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cup</Term> in its first two seasons, 2006 and 2007, under coach Dominic Kinnear, and reached two more finals in 2011 and 2012.
