### Fort Wayne (1941–1957)

Fred Zollner's Fort Wayne Zollner Pistons began in the National Basketball League in 1941 and joined the BAA, the forerunner of the NBA, in 1948. They reached the NBA Finals in 1955 and 1956 but lost both.

### Detroit's early years (1957–1981)

Zollner moved the team to Detroit in 1957, a much bigger city that had gone a decade without professional basketball. Dave Bing and Bob Lanier were the stars of the 1960s and 1970s.

### Isiah and the back-to-back titles (1981–1994)

Detroit drafted Isiah Thomas second overall in 1981, and Chuck Daly became coach in 1983. With Joe Dumars, Bill Laimbeer, Dennis Rodman and Vinnie Johnson, the Pistons swept the Lakers in the 1989 Finals and beat Portland in 1990. Dumars and then Thomas were named <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>.

### The 2004 title (2000–2008)

Ben Wallace, a four-time Defensive Player of the Year, anchored a team with Richard Hamilton that beat the Lakers in the 2004 Finals.

### Back downtown (2011–today)

Tom Gores bought the club in 2011, and in 2017 the Pistons moved to Little Caesars Arena in downtown Detroit. Led by Cade Cunningham, they are coming off a 60-win season.
