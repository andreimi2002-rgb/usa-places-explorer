title: Why this team matters
---

The Bills are the only team to reach four <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowls</Term> in a row, from the 1990 to the 1993 season, and they lost all four. Before that they won two AFL titles, in 1964 and 1965. Today they are led by the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Josh Allen, the 2024 <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term>.
