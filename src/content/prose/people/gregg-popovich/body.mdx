---
title: Career
---

Gregg Popovich became the Spurs' head coach during the 1996–97 season and stayed for 29 seasons. He won five titles, in 1999, 2003, 2005, 2007 and 2014, and 1,390 regular-season games.

He stepped down during the 2024–25 season and entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> as a coach in 2023.
