---
title: About the city
---

Washington has had its NFL team since 1937; today's Commanders play in Landover, Maryland, east of the city, and plan a new stadium in Washington for 2030. The franchise now called the Wizards has been here since 1973, first as the Capital Bullets and the Washington Bullets. D.C. United, a founding MLS club, won four of the first nine MLS Cups. Washington runs on Eastern Time, five hours behind London and seven behind Bucharest for most of the year.
