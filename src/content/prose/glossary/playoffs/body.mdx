---
title: Playoffs
---

The NBA regular season does not crown a champion on its own. Instead, the best teams in each conference go on to the playoffs, a knockout tournament played in series: you have to win four games against the same opponent to move on.

Before the main bracket, teams that finish seventh to tenth in each conference play a short Play-In Tournament for the last two places. The two conference champions then meet in the NBA Finals.

In the NFL every playoff round is a single game: seven teams from each conference qualify, and the two conference champions meet in the <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowl</Term>. MLS has its own format, the MLS Cup Playoffs, ending with the <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cup</Term> final.
