---
title: Why this team matters
---

Under coach Gregg Popovich, the Spurs won five NBA titles between 1999 and 2014, with Tim Duncan at the center of all of them.

For Europeans they are also easy to follow: their title teams featured France's Tony Parker and Argentina's Manu Ginóbili, and today's team is built around Victor Wembanyama, the French center drafted first overall in 2023. In 2026 they reached the <Term id="nba-finals">NBA Finals</Term> again, losing to New York.
