### A new team (1995–2010)

The Panthers began in 1995 and moved into their Charlotte stadium in 1996. With the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Jake Delhomme and the receiver Steve Smith they reached Super Bowl XXXVIII. The linebacker Sam Mills gave the club its motto, "Keep pounding".

### Cam Newton (2011–2019)

Cam Newton, the first pick of 2011, was MVP in 2015, when the Panthers went 15–1 and reached Super Bowl 50. The linebacker Luke Kuechly led the defence.

### Today (2020–today)

David Tepper bought the club in 2018. Bryce Young, the first pick of 2023, is the quarterback; under Dave Canales the Panthers won the NFC South in 2025 and lost a close playoff game to the Rams.
