title: History by eras
---

### New Orleans (1974–1979)

The Jazz joined the NBA in New Orleans in 1974. Pete Maravich was their star.

### Stockton, Malone and Sloan (1984–2003)

Utah drafted John Stockton in 1984 and Karl Malone in 1985. Jerry Sloan became head coach in 1988 and stayed until 2011. Malone was MVP in 1997 and 1999, and the Jazz reached the Finals in 1997 and 1998, losing both times to Chicago. Stockton finished his nineteen seasons with 15,806 assists.

### After the duo (2003–2022)

Deron Williams, Gordon Hayward, Donovan Mitchell and Rudy Gobert led later Jazz teams.

### A new core (2022–today)

The 2026–27 Jazz, coached by Will Hardy, are built around Lauri Markkanen, Jaren Jackson Jr. and Keyonte George, plus Darryn Peterson, the second pick of the 2026 draft.
