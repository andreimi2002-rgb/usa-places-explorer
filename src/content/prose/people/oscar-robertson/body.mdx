---
title: Career
---

Oscar Robertson spent ten seasons with the Cincinnati Royals, today's Sacramento Kings, and was Rookie of the Year in 1961 and league <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> in 1964. In 1970 he joined Milwaukee, and with Kareem Abdul-Jabbar he won the 1971 title. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 1980.
