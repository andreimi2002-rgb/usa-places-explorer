---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Miami Heat show how fast a new club can climb. Ted Arison brought the franchise to South Florida in 1988, as an <Term id="expansion-team">expansion team</Term>, and within eighteen years it was NBA champion. It has now won three titles and reached the <Term id="nba-finals">NBA Finals</Term> seven times.

Two people shaped most of that story. Pat Riley arrived in 1995 as coach and president and is still running the club, and Erik Spoelstra has been head coach since 2008. For a newcomer, the Heat are also the easiest way into one of the most talked-about periods in modern basketball: the four seasons when LeBron James, Dwyane Wade and Chris Bosh played together and reached the Finals every year.
