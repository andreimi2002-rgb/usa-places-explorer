---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Union joined MLS in 2010 and have become one of its best-run clubs, built on a youth academy that has sold players such as Brenden Aaronson to Europe. They won the <Term id="supporters-shield">Supporters' Shield</Term> in 2020 and 2025 and reached the 2022 <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cup</Term> final, lost on penalties to LAFC.
