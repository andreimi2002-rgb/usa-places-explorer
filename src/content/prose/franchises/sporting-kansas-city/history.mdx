### The Wizards (1996–2010)

Founded by Lamar Hunt, the club played its first match on April 13, 1996, as the Kansas City Wiz, and became the Wizards a year later. Preki was MVP in 1997 and 2003, and in 2000 the goalkeeper Tony Meola was MVP as the Wizards won both the <Term id="supporters-shield">Supporters' Shield</Term> and the <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cup</Term>.

### Sporting (2011–today)

Renamed Sporting Kansas City in November 2010, the club moved into its own stadium in 2011. With Matt Besler and Graham Zusi, it won the U.S. Open Cup in 2012 and the MLS Cup in 2013, on penalties against Real Salt Lake, and further Open Cups in 2015 and 2017. Raphaël Wicky became coach in January 2026.
