---
title: About the city
---

Denver, the "Mile High City", has had the Broncos since 1960 and the Nuggets since 1967, first in the ABA and since 1976 in the NBA. The Colorado Rapids, a founding MLS club, have played in Commerce City, north-east of Denver, since 2007. Denver runs on Mountain Time, seven hours behind London and nine behind Bucharest for most of the year.
