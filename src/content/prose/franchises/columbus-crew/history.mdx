### A founding club (1996–2006)

The Crew played MLS's first season in 1996 at Ohio Stadium, the university's football ground, with Brian McBride, the first pick of the first draft, in attack. In 1999 they opened Columbus Crew Stadium, the first purpose-built soccer stadium in the league, and won the U.S. Open Cup in 2002.

### The first title (2007–2016)

Guillermo Barros Schelotto arrived in 2007 and in 2008 was named MVP as the Crew won both the <Term id="supporters-shield">Supporters' Shield</Term> and their first MLS Cup.

### Saved and champions again (2017–today)

In October 2017 the owner, Anthony Precourt, announced a possible move to Austin. Fans campaigned to keep the club, Ohio's authorities went to court, and in 2019 a group led by the Haslam family bought the club and kept it in Columbus. The Crew won the MLS Cup in 2020, opened a new downtown stadium in 2021, won the MLS Cup again in 2023 and the Leagues Cup in 2024.
