---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Red Bulls are the New York area's original MLS club. They began in 1996 as the MetroStars and were bought by Red Bull, the drinks company, in 2006. They have won the <Term id="supporters-shield">Supporters' Shield</Term> three times, in 2013, 2015 and 2018, but are still waiting for an <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cup</Term>: they lost the finals of 2008 and 2024.
