---
title: Career
---

Dallas drafted Jason Kidd second in 1994, and he was Rookie of the Year. He played for Phoenix, New Jersey and New York, returned to Dallas in 2008 and won the 2011 title there. He later coached the Mavericks from 2021 to 2026, reaching the 2024 Finals, and entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> as a player in 2018.
