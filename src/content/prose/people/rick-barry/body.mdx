---
title: Career
---

San Francisco drafted Rick Barry fourth overall in 1965, and he was Rookie of the Year. He then spent several seasons in the rival ABA before returning to the Warriors in 1972.

In 1975 he led Golden State to the title with a four-game sweep of Washington and was named <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 1987.
