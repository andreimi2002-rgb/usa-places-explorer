---
title: About the city
---

New Orleans has had the Saints since 1967; they won Super Bowl XLIV in the 2009 season, four years after Hurricane Katrina. The Jazz played here from 1974 to 1979 before moving to Utah, and the Hornets arrived from Charlotte in 2002 and were renamed the Pelicans in 2013. Gayle Benson owns both the Saints and the Pelicans. New Orleans runs on Central Time, six hours behind London and eight behind Bucharest for most of the year.
