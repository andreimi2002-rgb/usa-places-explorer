---
title: Career in Orlando
---

Orlando took Shaquille O'Neal with the first pick of the 1992 <Term id="nba-draft">draft</Term>, and he changed the young club overnight. A center of rare size and speed, he was named Rookie of the Year in his first season and, with Penny Hardaway beside him, led Orlando to the 1995 NBA Finals.

His stay lasted four seasons. In July 1996 he signed with the Los Angeles Lakers as a <Term id="free-agency">free agent</Term>, and Orlando had to start again. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2016 and the Magic's own Hall of Fame in 2015.
