---
title: Career
---

Kobe Bryant was drafted 13th overall by Charlotte in 1996 and started his rookie season with the Lakers at 18. He played all twenty seasons of his career in Los Angeles.

With Shaquille O'Neal he won three titles in a row, in 2000, 2001 and 2002. After the Lakers traded for Pau Gasol, he led them to two more, in 2009 and 2010, and was <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term> both times. He was league <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> in 2008 and scored 60 points in his final game in 2016. He died on January 26, 2020, and entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> that year.
