### Portsmouth and the early titles (1930–1957)

The club began in the NFL in 1930 as the Portsmouth Spartans, from a small Ohio town, and moved to Detroit in 1934. It won the 1935 championship and, with the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Bobby Layne, those of 1952, 1953 and 1957.

### The long wait (1958–2020)

The Ford family has owned the club since 1964. Barry Sanders, MVP in 1997, and Calvin Johnson were stars of teams that rarely won in the playoffs. Ford Field opened in 2002.

### Campbell (2021–today)

Dan Campbell arrived in 2021, and Jared Goff became the quarterback the same year. The Lions reached the NFC final in the 2023 season, with Amon-Ra St. Brown and Aidan Hutchinson among their stars.
