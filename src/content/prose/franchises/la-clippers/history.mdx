### Buffalo Braves (1970–1978)

The franchise joined the NBA in 1970 as the Buffalo Braves. Bob McAdoo, drafted second in 1972, led the league in scoring three years in a row and was <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> in 1975.

### San Diego Clippers (1978–1984)

The team moved to San Diego in 1978 and became the Clippers; Bill Walton played there in the early 1980s.

### Los Angeles (1984–today)

The Clippers moved to Los Angeles in 1984. Blake Griffin, the first pick of 2009, and Chris Paul, who arrived in 2011, led the team in the 2010s. Ballmer completed his purchase in August 2014, Kawhi Leonard and Paul George arrived in 2019, and the team moved into the Intuit Dome in 2024. Coached by Tyronn Lue, the 2026–27 Clippers are built around Darius Garland and Brandon Ingram.
