---
title: Why this team matters
---

Milwaukee has two NBA titles, fifty years apart. The first came in 1971, only the club's third season, with Kareem Abdul-Jabbar and Oscar Robertson. The second came in 2021, led by Giannis Antetokounmpo.

In July 2026 the Bucks traded Antetokounmpo to Miami and began a new era around Tyler Herro and young players.
