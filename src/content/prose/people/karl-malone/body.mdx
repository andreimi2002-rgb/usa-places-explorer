---
title: Career
---

Utah drafted Karl Malone 13th in 1985, and he played eighteen seasons for the Jazz alongside John Stockton. He was <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> in 1997 and 1999, reached the Finals twice, and finished his career with the Lakers in 2003–04 with 36,928 points. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2010.
