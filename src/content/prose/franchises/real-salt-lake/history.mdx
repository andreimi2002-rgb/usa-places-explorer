---

### Kreis's team (2005–2013)

Real Salt Lake's first player, Jason Kreis, scored its first goal in 2005 and in 2007 retired to become coach. In 2008 the club moved into its own stadium in Sandy. A year later, with Beckerman, Rimando and the Argentine Javier Morales, it won the MLS Cup; Rimando was MVP of the final. RSL lost the 2011 CONCACAF Champions League final and the 2013 MLS Cup final on penalties to Sporting Kansas City.

### After the golden era (2014–today)

Beckerman played until 2020, more regular-season games than any other outfield player in MLS history, and Rimando until 2019. Gail Miller's group bought the club in April 2025.
