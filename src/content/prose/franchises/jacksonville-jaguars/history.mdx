### Expansion (1995–2011)

The Jaguars began in 1995 under Tom Coughlin, with the tackle Tony Boselli and the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Mark Brunell, and reached the AFC final in only their second season.

### Shad Khan and London (2012–2024)

Shad Khan bought the club in 2012 and the Jaguars began their London series in 2013. Trevor Lawrence was the first pick of the 2021 draft.

### Today (2025–today)

Liam Coen became coach in 2025 and took the team from 4–13 to 13–4 and a division title. The two-way player Travis Hunter, the 2024 Heisman Trophy winner, joined the same year.
