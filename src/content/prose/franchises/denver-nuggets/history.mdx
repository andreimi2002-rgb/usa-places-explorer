### ABA beginnings (1967–1976)

The club was founded in the ABA in 1967 as the Denver Rockets and took the name Nuggets in 1974. Two years later it was one of four ABA teams to join the NBA.

### Alex English and the 1980s (1979–1990)

Alex English, who arrived in 1980, led the league in scoring in 1983.

### Carmelo Anthony (2003–2011)

Carmelo Anthony played for Denver from 2003 to 2011.

### Jokić and the title (2014–today)

Denver drafted Nikola Jokić in the second round in 2014. He was named MVP in 2021, 2022 and 2024, and in 2023, with Jamal Murray and Aaron Gordon, the Nuggets beat Miami 4–1 for their first NBA title. Jokić was <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>.
