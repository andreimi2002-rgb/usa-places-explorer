---
title: About the city
---

Toronto has had the Blue Jays, Major League Baseball's only Canadian team, since 1977; they won the World Series in 1992 and 1993. The Raptors arrived in 1995 and won the 2019 NBA title, and Toronto FC, the first Canadian MLS club, began in 2007 and won the 2017 MLS Cup. Toronto runs on Eastern Time, five hours behind London and seven behind Bucharest for most of the year.
