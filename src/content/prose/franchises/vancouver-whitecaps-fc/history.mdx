### The old Whitecaps (1974–2010)

The original Whitecaps played in the North American Soccer League from 1974 to 1984 and won Soccer Bowl '79. A lower-league club kept the name alive until MLS.

### MLS (2011–today)

The Whitecaps joined MLS in 2011. Camilo Sanvezzo won the Golden Boot in 2013, and the teenage Alphonso Davies came through the club before leaving for Bayern Munich after the 2018 season. Vancouver won the Canadian Championship in 2015 and every year from 2022 to 2025. In 2025, under the coach Jesper Sørensen and with Müller arriving in August, it reached two continental and league finals.
