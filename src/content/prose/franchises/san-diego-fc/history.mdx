### A record debut (2023–today)

MLS awarded San Diego its place on May 18, 2023. Coached by Mikey Varas, the team began in 2025 at Snapdragon Stadium; the Danish winger Anders Dreyer scored twice in its first match and was named Newcomer of the Year as the club set an expansion record of 63 points.
