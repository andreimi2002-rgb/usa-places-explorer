### Seattle SuperSonics (1967–2008)

The SuperSonics started in Seattle in 1967. Coached by Lenny Wilkens, they won the 1979 title, with Dennis Johnson named <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>. In the 1990s Gary Payton and Shawn Kemp took them to the 1996 Finals, which they lost to Chicago.

### The move (2006–2008)

In October 2006 a group led by Oklahoma businessman Clay Bennett bought the team. In 2007 it drafted Kevin Durant second overall, and in July 2008 the franchise moved to Oklahoma City. The Thunder name was unveiled on September 3, 2008.

### Durant, Westbrook and Harden (2008–2019)

With Durant, Russell Westbrook and James Harden, the young Thunder reached the 2012 Finals and lost to Miami. Durant left in 2016; Westbrook stayed until 2019 and was named <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> in 2017.

### Gilgeous-Alexander and the 2025 title (2019–today)

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander arrived in 2019. In 2025 he was league MVP and Finals MVP as the Thunder beat Indiana 103–91 in Game 7 for their first title in Oklahoma City, and he was named MVP again in 2026.
