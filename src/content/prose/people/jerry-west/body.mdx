---
title: Career
---

The Lakers drafted Jerry West second overall in 1960, the year they moved to Los Angeles, and he played all fourteen seasons of his career there. He reached the Finals many times but won only once, in 1972.

In 1969 he became the first ever <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>, even though his team lost the series to Boston. He later coached the Lakers for three seasons. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> as a player in 1980 and again as a contributor in 2024, and died on June 12, 2024.
