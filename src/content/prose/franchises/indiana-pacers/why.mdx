---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Pacers began in the ABA, a rival league, in 1967, and won its title in 1970, 1972 and 1973. They joined the NBA in 1976, along with the Spurs, Nuggets and Nets.

In the NBA they are still waiting for a title. They reached the Finals in 2000, with Reggie Miller, and in 2025, when they took Oklahoma City to a seventh game.
