---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Red Sox won five of the first fifteen <Term id="world-series">World Series</Term>, then sold Babe Ruth to the Yankees in 1920 and waited 86 years for the next one: the "Curse of the Bambino". It ended in 2004, when Boston came back from 0–3 down against the Yankees in the semi-final and won the title. They have won nine in all and play at Fenway Park, the oldest ballpark in the major leagues. Their owners also own Liverpool FC.
