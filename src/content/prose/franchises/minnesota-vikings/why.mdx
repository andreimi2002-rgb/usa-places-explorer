---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Vikings, named for Minnesota's Scandinavian heritage, are one of the NFL's most successful teams without a <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowl</Term>: they won the 1969 NFL championship and then lost Super Bowls IV, VIII, IX and XI. Their owners, the Wilf family, also own Orlando City in MLS. The receiver Justin Jefferson is their biggest star.
