---
title: About the city
---

Baltimore has had baseball's Orioles since 1954, when they arrived from St. Louis, and the NFL's Ravens since 1996; both have won titles, three World Series and two Super Bowls. The NFL's Colts played here from 1953 until they moved to Indianapolis in 1984, and the NBA's Bullets, today's Washington Wizards, from 1963 to 1973. Baltimore runs on Eastern Time, five hours behind London and seven behind Bucharest for most of the year.
