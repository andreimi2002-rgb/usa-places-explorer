title: Why this team matters
---

The Rams have moved more than most clubs: from Cleveland to Los Angeles in 1946, to St. Louis in 1995 and back to Los Angeles in 2016. They won <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowl</Term> XXXIV in St. Louis and Super Bowl LVI at home, in their own stadium. In 2025 their <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Matthew Stafford was named <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term>.
