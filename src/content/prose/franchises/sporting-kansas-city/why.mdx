---
title: Why this team matters
---

Sporting Kansas City, the Kansas City Wizards until 2010, is one of MLS's founding clubs and a two-time champion, in 2000 and 2013. Its stadium in Kansas City, Kansas, opened in 2011, and under coach Peter Vermes from 2009 to 2025 the club won the MLS Cup and three U.S. Open Cups.
