### Lower-league beginnings (2010–2014)

Orlando City was founded in 2010 in the USL, a lower division, and won two league titles there, in 2011 and 2013, while campaigning for a place in MLS.

### Into MLS (2015–2019)

The club entered MLS in 2015, together with New York City FC, and sold its USL place to Louisville. Kaká was the face of the team and the young Canadian Cyle Larin won Rookie of the Year. In 2017 Orlando moved into its own stadium west of downtown.

### Contenders (2020–today)

Orlando reached the <Term id="playoffs">playoffs</Term> for the first time in 2020, with Nani in the side. The Wilf family bought the club in 2021. Orlando won the U.S. Open Cup in 2022 and reached the Eastern Conference final for the first time in 2024, losing to the New York Red Bulls. In 2026 the club signed Antoine Griezmann.
