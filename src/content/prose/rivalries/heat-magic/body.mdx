---
title: Why this rivalry matters
---

The Heat and the Magic are Florida's two NBA teams, and both were born in the same burst of expansion: Miami started play in 1988 and Orlando a season later. That makes this a rivalry of neighbors who grew up together, and today they share the Southeast Division, so they meet every regular season.

### How it started

The first meeting came on November 28, 1989, in Orlando, and the Magic won 104–99. In those early years both clubs were learning to compete as <Term id="expansion-team">expansion teams</Term>, so a win over the other new team in the state meant more than the standings suggested.

### The one playoff series

The teams have met in the <Term id="playoffs">playoffs</Term> only once, in the first round in 1997, and it is the heart of the rivalry. Miami was the stronger side and won the first two games easily. Then Penny Hardaway took over in Orlando, scoring 42 points in Game 3 and 41 in Game 4 to win both and force a deciding fifth game. Miami won that game at home, 91–83, to take the series 3–2.

### Why it still matters

The Heat have since won NBA titles while the Magic are still waiting for their first, which adds an edge to every meeting. For a visitor it is an easy rivalry to follow: one state, two very different cities, and a short history that you can learn in an evening.
