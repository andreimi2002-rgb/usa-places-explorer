---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Orioles began in Milwaukee in 1901, spent half a century as the St. Louis Browns and moved to Baltimore in 1954. There they became one of the American League's best clubs, winning three <Term id="world-series">World Series</Term> with players like Brooks Robinson, Frank Robinson and Jim Palmer. Cal Ripken Jr. played 2,632 games in a row for them, a record.
