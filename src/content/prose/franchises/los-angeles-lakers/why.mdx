---
title: Why this team matters
---

Few clubs in any sport have a history like the Lakers'. They have won 17 NBA titles, second only to Boston's 18, and reached the <Term id="nba-finals">NBA Finals</Term> 32 times. Their story also crosses the country: the team began in Minneapolis in 1947 and moved to Los Angeles in 1960, keeping a name that made more sense among Minnesota's lakes than in California.

For a European, the Lakers are a tour of basketball history through its biggest names: George Mikan in the 1950s, Jerry West and Elgin Baylor in the 1960s, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar and Magic Johnson in the "Showtime" 1980s, Shaquille O'Neal and Kobe Bryant around 2000, and LeBron James more recently.
