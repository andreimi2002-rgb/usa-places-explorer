---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Steelers have belonged to the Rooney family since Art Rooney founded them in 1933. They have won six <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowls</Term>, tied for the most, four of them in the 1970s with the "Steel Curtain" defence under Chuck Noll. Their coaches have stayed for decades: only three led the team from 1969 to 2025.
