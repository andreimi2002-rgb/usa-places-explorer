---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Cubs are one of the two clubs left from the National League's first season, 1876. They won the <Term id="world-series">World Series</Term> in 1907 and 1908 and then waited 108 years, until 2016, the longest drought in American sport. They play at Wrigley Field, with its ivy-covered walls, their home since 1916.
