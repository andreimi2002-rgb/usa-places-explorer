---
title: How the NBA works
---

The NBA is a closed league: teams are franchises that belong to the league, and there is no promotion or relegation. New cities join as <Term id="expansion-team">expansion teams</Term>, and existing franchises sometimes move, keeping their history but changing their name or city.

The teams are split into two conferences, Eastern and Western, and each conference into three divisions. Teams play a long regular season, then the best go on to the <Term id="playoffs">playoffs</Term>, which end with the <Term id="nba-finals">NBA Finals</Term> between the two conference champions.

Talent is shared out through the <Term id="nba-draft">draft</Term>, where weaker teams get better chances at the top young players, and moves between teams happen through <Term id="trade">trades</Term> and <Term id="free-agency">free agency</Term> rather than transfer fees.
