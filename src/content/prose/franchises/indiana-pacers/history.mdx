### The ABA dynasty (1967–1976)

The Pacers were founded in 1967 for the first season of the ABA. With Mel Daniels and Roger Brown they won three ABA titles in four seasons, in 1970, 1972 and 1973. In 1976 they were one of four ABA clubs to join the NBA.

### Reggie Miller (1987–2005)

Indiana drafted Reggie Miller 11th overall in 1987, and he spent all eighteen seasons of his career there. Herb and Melvin Simon had bought the team in 1983. With Rik Smits at center, the Pacers reached the 2000 Finals and lost to the Lakers.

### Haliburton and the 2025 Finals (2022–today)

Tyrese Haliburton arrived in 2022. With Pascal Siakam, Andrew Nembhard and Aaron Nesmith, the Pacers reached the 2025 Finals and lost to Oklahoma City in seven games. Coached by Rick Carlisle, they return the same core in 2026–27, plus Ivica Zubac.
