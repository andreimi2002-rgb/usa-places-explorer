### Syracuse Nationals (1949–1963)

The franchise was born in 1949 as the Syracuse Nationals. Led by Dolph Schayes, it won the 1955 title, beating Fort Wayne in seven games. In 1963 Irv Kosloff, a Philadelphia native, bought the team and moved it to Philadelphia, where it was renamed the 76ers.

### Wilt and the 1967 title (1965–1968)

Wilt Chamberlain joined in 1965, and in 1967 the 76ers beat San Francisco in six games for their second title.

### Julius Erving and Moses Malone (1976–1987)

Julius Erving, a three-time <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> in the ABA, arrived in 1976 and was NBA MVP in 1981. In 1982 Philadelphia added Moses Malone, and the 1983 team swept the Lakers 4–0, with Malone as <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>.

### Iverson (1996–2010)

The 76ers drafted Allen Iverson first overall in 1996. In 2001 he was league MVP and led the team to the Finals, which they lost to the Lakers.

### Embiid and the 2026 roster (2014–today)

Joel Embiid, drafted third in 2014, was MVP in 2023. In the summer of 2026 Philadelphia traded for Jaylen Brown and signed LeBron James, now in his 24th season.
