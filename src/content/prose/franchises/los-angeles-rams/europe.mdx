title: For European fans

Stan Kroenke, the Rams' owner, also owns Arsenal in the Premier League. The Rams have played in London five times: at Wembley in 2012, 2019 and 2025, and at Twickenham in 2016 and 2017. In September 2026 they played the first NFL regular-season game in Australia, in Melbourne. Los Angeles is nine hours behind Central Europe.
