---
title: About the city
---

Tampa lies on the Gulf coast of Florida, at the head of Tampa Bay. The Buccaneers have played here since 1976. Baseball's Rays play across the bay in St. Petersburg, at Tropicana Field; after a hurricane damaged it in 2024, they spent the 2025 season at Steinbrenner Field in Tampa. Tampa runs on Eastern Time, five hours behind London and seven behind Bucharest for most of the year.
