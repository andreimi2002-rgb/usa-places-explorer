---
title: Career
---

Wilt Chamberlain joined the Philadelphia Warriors in 1959 and moved with them to San Francisco. He once scored 100 points in a single game, and was named league <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> four times.

He won titles with the Philadelphia 76ers in 1967 and the Lakers in 1972, when he was <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 1979 and died on October 12, 1999.
