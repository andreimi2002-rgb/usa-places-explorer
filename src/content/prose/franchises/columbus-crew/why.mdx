---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Crew are one of MLS's ten founding clubs and the league's soccer pioneers: in 1999 they opened the first stadium built for an MLS team. They have won three <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cups</Term>, in 2008, 2020 and 2023, and their fans' campaign to stop a move to Texas in 2017–18 is one of the best-known stories in American sport.
