---
title: Career
---

George Gervin started in the ABA and joined the Spurs in 1974, when they were still an ABA club. He stayed when they joined the NBA in 1976 and led the league in scoring four times.

He finished his career with Chicago in 1985–86 and entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 1996.
