---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Thunder are one franchise with two cities. It began in 1967 as the Seattle SuperSonics, won the 1979 title there, and moved to Oklahoma City in 2008. In 2025, led by Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, it won its first title as the Thunder.

For a European it is a clear example of how American franchises can relocate, something almost unknown in European football.
