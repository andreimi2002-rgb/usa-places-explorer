---
title: About the city
---

Seattle has had the Seahawks since 1976 and the Sounders in MLS since 2009; both play at Lumen Field. The Seahawks won Super Bowl LX in February 2026, and the Sounders won the MLS Cup in 2016 and 2019. The NBA's SuperSonics played here from 1967 to 2008 and won the 1979 title before moving to Oklahoma City. Seattle runs on Pacific Time, eight hours behind London and ten behind Bucharest for most of the year.
