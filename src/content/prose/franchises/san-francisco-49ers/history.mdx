### The AAFC and Kezar (1946–1978)

Founded in 1944, the 49ers began play in 1946 in the All-America Football Conference and joined the NFL in 1950. They played at Kezar Stadium and from 1971 at Candlestick Park without winning a title.

### The dynasty (1979–1998)

Bill Walsh was hired in 1979, the year Montana was drafted. San Francisco won Super Bowls XVI, XIX, XXIII and XXIV, then XXIX with Steve Young.

### Santa Clara (2014–today)

The 49ers moved into Levi's Stadium in Silicon Valley in 2014. Under Kyle Shanahan, coach since 2017, they reached Super Bowls LIV and LVIII and lost both to Kansas City. Brock Purdy, the last pick of the 2022 <Term id="nfl-draft">draft</Term>, became the quarterback.
