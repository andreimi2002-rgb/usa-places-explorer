---

### Instant champions (2006–2012)

With Brian Ching and Dwayne De Rosario, Houston beat New England in both the 2006 and 2007 finals; Ching and De Rosario were the MLS Cup MVPs. The club played at the University of Houston's Robertson Stadium and lost the 2011 and 2012 finals to the LA Galaxy.

### A new stadium (2012–today)

In 2012 the Dynamo opened their own downtown stadium. They won the U.S. Open Cup in 2018 and 2023, became Houston Dynamo FC in 2020 and are coached in 2026 by Ben Olsen, the former D.C. United midfielder.
