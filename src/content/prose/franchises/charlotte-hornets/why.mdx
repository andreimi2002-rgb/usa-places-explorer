---
title: Why this team matters
---

Charlotte's NBA story has a gap in the middle. The original Hornets joined the league in 1988 and moved to New Orleans in 2002. Two years later a new team, the Charlotte Bobcats, began play, and in 2014 it took back the Hornets name and the original team's history.

Michael Jordan owned the club from 2010 to 2023, when he sold his majority stake. The Hornets have not yet reached the <Term id="nba-finals">NBA Finals</Term>.
