---

### The first years (2010–2013)

The Union played their first home match on April 10, 2010, and Sébastien Le Toux scored the club's first goal. That June they moved into their own stadium on the river at Chester. The Jamaican goalkeeper Andre Blake arrived as a first overall draft pick and became Goalkeeper of the Year in 2016.

### The Curtin era (2014–2024)

Under Jim Curtin, coach from 2014 to 2024, and with the captain Alejandro Bedoya, the Union won the Supporters' Shield in 2020 and reached the 2022 MLS Cup final, losing to LAFC on penalties after a 3–3 draw. Their YSC Academy, founded in 2013, produced players such as Brenden Aaronson, sold to Red Bull Salzburg in 2021.

### Today (2025–today)

Dániel Gazdag, the club's top scorer, was sold to Columbus in April 2025, but the Union won a second Supporters' Shield that year.
