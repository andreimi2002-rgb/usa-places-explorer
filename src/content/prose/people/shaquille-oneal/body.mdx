---
title: Career
---

Orlando took Shaquille O'Neal with the first pick of the 1992 <Term id="nba-draft">draft</Term>, and he changed the young club overnight. A center of rare size and speed, he was named Rookie of the Year in his first season and, with Penny Hardaway beside him, led Orlando to the 1995 NBA Finals.

In July 1996 he signed with the Los Angeles Lakers as a <Term id="free-agency">free agent</Term>. With Kobe Bryant he won three titles in a row, in 2000, 2001 and 2002, and was <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term> each time. He moved to Miami in 2004 and won a fourth title there in 2006, and he finished his career with Phoenix, Cleveland and Boston. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2016 and the Magic's own Hall of Fame in 2015.
