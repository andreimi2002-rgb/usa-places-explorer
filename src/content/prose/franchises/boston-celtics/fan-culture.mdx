---
title: City and fan culture
---

Boston has had the Celtics since the league's first season, 1946–47, and the team has never moved or changed its name. That continuity is part of the appeal: the same club, in the same city, owns the longest list of championships in the NBA.

Boston runs on Eastern Time, five hours behind London and seven behind Bucharest for most of the year, so a 7:30 PM game starts at half past midnight in London.
