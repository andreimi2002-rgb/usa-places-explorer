---
title: Career
---

Moses Malone was already a two-time MVP when he joined Philadelphia in 1982. In his first season there he was MVP again and led the 76ers to the 1983 title, sweeping the Lakers, as <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>.

He returned to Philadelphia for the 1993–94 season and finished his career with San Antonio. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2001 and died on September 13, 2015.
