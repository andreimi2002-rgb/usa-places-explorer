### Brooklyn (1883–1957)

The club was founded in Brooklyn in 1883 and had several names before settling on the Dodgers in 1932. Jackie Robinson arrived in 1947, and the "Boys of Summer", with Duke Snider, won the 1955 World Series.

### Los Angeles (1958–2011)

The team moved to Los Angeles in 1958 and opened Dodger Stadium in 1962. Sandy Koufax and Don Drysdale led the 1960s champions, and the Mexican <Term id="pitcher">pitcher</Term> Fernando Valenzuela the 1981 team; Tommy Lasorda managed for 21 seasons.

### Today (2012–today)

Guggenheim Baseball Management, including Magic Johnson, bought the club in 2012. Under Dave Roberts, manager since 2016, the Dodgers won the World Series in 2020, 2024 and 2025. In 2026 they reached the postseason for the 14th season in a row, equalling the major league record.
