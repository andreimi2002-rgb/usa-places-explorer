### Bud Grant and the Purple People Eaters (1961–1985)

The Vikings began in 1961. Bud Grant, coach from 1967, built a famous defensive line, the "Purple People Eaters", with Alan Page and Carl Eller; Page was the first defender named league MVP. With the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Fran Tarkenton they reached four Super Bowls and lost them all.

### Moss, Carter and Peterson (1986–2021)

The Vikings moved indoors to the Metrodome in 1982. Randy Moss and Cris Carter, and later Adrian Peterson, MVP in 2012, starred, but the team lost its NFC finals.

### Today (2022–today)

Kevin O'Connell became coach in 2022. The team has played at U.S. Bank Stadium since 2016, and in 2026 it signed the quarterback Kyler Murray from Arizona.
