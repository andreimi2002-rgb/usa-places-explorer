---
title: About the city
---

New York has had the Knicks since the NBA's first season and the NFL's Giants since 1925; the Giants have played across the river in New Jersey since 1976. The Nets moved to Brooklyn in 2012. In soccer, the Red Bulls, a founding MLS club, play in Harrison, New Jersey, and New York City FC joined in 2015. New York City runs on Eastern Time, five hours behind London and seven behind Bucharest for most of the year.
