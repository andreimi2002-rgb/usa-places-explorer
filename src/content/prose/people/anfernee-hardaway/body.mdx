### The trade

Anfernee "Penny" Hardaway, from Memphis, Tennessee, starred at Memphis State before the 1993 <Term id="nba-draft">draft</Term>. Orlando held the first pick and chose Chris Webber, then traded him on draft night to Golden State for Hardaway, taken third, and three future first-round picks. It became one of the most talked-about trades of the decade.

### A star guard (1993–1999)

At 6 feet 7 inches (2.01 m), Hardaway was a point guard with a forward's size: he could run the offence, score from anywhere and defend bigger players. With Shaquille O'Neal he formed one of the league's most exciting young duos. He was named to the <Term id="all-nba">All-NBA</Term> First Team in 1995 and 1996, the year Orlando reached the Finals and then won 60 games.

After O'Neal left in 1996, Hardaway became the face of the club. In the 1997 <Term id="playoffs">playoffs</Term> against Miami he scored 42 and 41 points in back-to-back games, though the Heat won the series. In 1999 he moved on to Phoenix, later playing for New York and Miami.

### Legacy

Hardaway was a four-time <Term id="all-star">All-Star</Term> and a three-time All-NBA pick. He joined the Magic's Hall of Fame in 2017.
