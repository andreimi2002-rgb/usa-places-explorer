### The Miracle Mets (1962–1979)

The Mets lost 120 games in their first season. With the <Term id="pitcher">pitcher</Term> Tom Seaver they won the 1969 World Series, and they reached it again in 1973.

### 1986 (1980–1999)

Dwight Gooden, Darryl Strawberry and Keith Hernandez led the team that won the 1986 World Series.

### Citi Field (2000–today)

The Mets lost the 2000 World Series to the Yankees and the 2015 one to Kansas City. They moved into Citi Field in 2009. Steve Cohen bought the club in 2020 and signed Juan Soto in 2025 on a record contract; Andy Green replaced Carlos Mendoza as manager in June 2026.
