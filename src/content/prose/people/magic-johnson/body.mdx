---
title: Career
---

The Lakers took Earvin "Magic" Johnson first overall in 1979. A 6-foot-9 point guard, unusually tall for the position, he ran the fast, flowing style that made the 1980s Lakers famous as "Showtime".

He won five titles, in 1980, 1982, 1985, 1987 and 1988, was <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term> three times and league <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> three times. He played his whole career with the Lakers and entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2002.
