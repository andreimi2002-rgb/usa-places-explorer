---
title: Why this team matters
---

The 49ers were the team of the 1980s. Under the coach Bill Walsh, with the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Joe Montana and the receiver Jerry Rice, they won four <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowls</Term> between the 1981 and 1989 seasons, and a fifth with Steve Young in the 1994 season. Walsh's short-passing "West Coast offence" was copied across the league.
