---
title: All-Star
---

Every February the NBA stops for All-Star weekend. Its centrepiece is the All-Star Game, an exhibition between the league's best and most popular players, chosen through a mix of fan, player, media and coaches' votes.

The game itself matters little, but selection does: "a seven-time All-Star" is the usual shorthand for a player who was among the league's elite for seven seasons. The best player of the game is named All-Star Game <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term>.
