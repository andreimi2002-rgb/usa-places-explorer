### ABA beginnings (1967–1976)

The franchise began in 1967 as the Dallas Chaparrals of the ABA, a rival league. In 1973 it was sold to local investors and moved to San Antonio as the Spurs. George Gervin became its star, and in 1976 the Spurs were one of four ABA clubs to join the NBA.

### Robinson and the first titles (1987–2003)

San Antonio drafted David Robinson first overall in 1987, and he joined the team in 1989, becoming Rookie of the Year. Gregg Popovich took over as head coach during the 1996–97 season, and in 1997 the Spurs drafted Tim Duncan first overall. With Robinson and Duncan together they won their first title in 1999, and their second in 2003.

### The Duncan, Parker and Ginóbili years (2003–2016)

Duncan, Tony Parker and Manu Ginóbili won three more titles, in 2005, 2007 and 2014. The 2014 win came against Miami, a year after the Heat had beaten them in seven games. Duncan played all nineteen of his seasons in San Antonio.

### Wembanyama (2023–today)

In 2023 the Spurs drafted Victor Wembanyama first overall. He was Rookie of the Year and, in 2025–26, Defensive Player of the Year. Popovich stepped down during the 2024–25 season and Mitch Johnson took over; in 2026 the Spurs reached the Finals and lost to the Knicks in five games.
