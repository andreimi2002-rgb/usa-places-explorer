---
title: Career
---

In 1985 the Knicks won the first ever <Term id="draft-lottery">draft lottery</Term> and chose Patrick Ewing, a center, first overall. He was Rookie of the Year and played fifteen seasons in New York, becoming the club's all-time leader in points, rebounds, blocks and steals.

He led the Knicks to the 1994 Finals, which they lost to Houston in seven games. He finished his career with Seattle and Orlando, and entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2008.
