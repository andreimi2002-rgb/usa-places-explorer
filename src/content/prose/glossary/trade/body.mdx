---
title: Trade
---

In European football, clubs mostly buy and sell players for transfer fees. In the NBA, the normal way to move a player is a trade: teams exchange players, draft picks or both, and the contracts move with the players. Salary rules limit which trades are allowed.

Trades can involve more than two teams. When Orlando moved Dwight Howard to the Lakers in 2012, four clubs took part, and Orlando received six players and five future draft picks in return.

In the NFL, draft picks are traded as often as players. In 2026 the Los Angeles Rams paid mostly in picks to get Myles Garrett from Cleveland and Trent McDuffie from Kansas City.
