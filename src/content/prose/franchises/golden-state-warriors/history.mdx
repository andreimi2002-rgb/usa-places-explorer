---

### Philadelphia (1946–1962)

The club began as the Philadelphia Warriors in 1946 and beat the Chicago Stags in the first Finals, in 1947. With Paul Arizin it won again in 1956. Wilt Chamberlain joined in 1959 and once scored 100 points in a game.

### San Francisco and Oakland (1962–2009)

The Warriors moved to San Francisco in 1962, and in 1971 they crossed the Bay to Oakland and became the Golden State Warriors. Rick Barry led them to the 1975 title with a four-game sweep of Washington and was named <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>.

### The Curry era (2009–today)

Golden State drafted Stephen Curry seventh overall in 2009. Joe Lacob and Peter Guber bought the club in 2010, and Steve Kerr, a former Bulls guard, became head coach in 2014. The Warriors won the 2015 title, their first in 40 years. In 2015–16 they went 73–9, a new record, and Curry became the first unanimous <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term>, but they lost the Finals to Cleveland.

Kevin Durant joined in 2016, and the Warriors won in 2017 and 2018. In 2019 they moved back to San Francisco, into the privately financed Chase Center. In 2022 they won a fourth title in eight seasons, with Curry as Finals MVP.
