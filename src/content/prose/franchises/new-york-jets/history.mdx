### The Titans and Namath (1960–1976)

The club began in 1960 as the New York Titans of the AFL and became the Jets in 1963, when Weeb Ewbank was hired. Namath signed in 1965, and with Don Maynard the Jets won the 1968 AFL title and Super Bowl III.

### Long wait (1977–2024)

The "New York Sack Exchange" defensive line of the early 1980s, Curtis Martin and Darrelle Revis followed, but no Super Bowl. The Jets played at Shea Stadium, then in New Jersey, and since 2010 at MetLife Stadium with the Giants.

### Today (2025–today)

Aaron Glenn, a former Jets cornerback, became coach in 2025. After a 3–14 season, the Jets are trying to end a playoff drought that goes back to the 2010 season.
