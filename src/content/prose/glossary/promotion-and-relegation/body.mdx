---
title: Promotion and relegation
---

In European football the bottom clubs of a league drop to the division below and the best clubs of that division move up. American leagues do not work this way. MLS is a closed league: a club gets in by being awarded an <Term id="expansion-team">expansion</Term> place, and no club can be relegated, however badly it plays. A bad season is punished differently, with an early end to the year and, in most leagues, a better draft pick.
