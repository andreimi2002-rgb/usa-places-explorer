### The AFL and the Orange Crush (1960–1982)

Founded in 1959 as a charter member of the AFL, Denver joined the NFL in the 1970 merger. Its "Orange Crush" defence took the club to Super Bowl XII.

### Elway (1983–1998)

John Elway arrived in a trade in 1983. He lost three Super Bowls, then won XXXII and XXXIII, with Terrell Davis running the ball, and retired.

### Manning and Super Bowl 50 (2012–2015)

Peyton Manning signed in 2012. Denver lost Super Bowl XLVIII to Seattle, then won Super Bowl 50 over Carolina, with the linebacker Von Miller as MVP.

### Today (2022–today)

A group led by Rob Walton bought the club in 2022. Sean Payton became coach in 2023 and Bo Nix quarterback in 2024. In 2025 Denver went 14–3 and lost the AFC final 10–7 to New England.
