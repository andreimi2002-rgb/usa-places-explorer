---
title: MLS Cup
---

The MLS Cup is the league's championship match, the last game of the <Term id="playoffs">playoffs</Term>. Unlike a European league, the champion is not the team with the most points: that team wins the <Term id="supporters-shield">Supporters' Shield</Term>, and the title is decided in a knockout tournament afterwards. The final is a single match, hosted by the finalist with the better regular-season record.
