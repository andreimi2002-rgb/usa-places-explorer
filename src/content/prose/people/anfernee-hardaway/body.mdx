---
title: Career in Orlando
---

Penny Hardaway reached Orlando through a famous draft-night <Term id="trade">trade</Term> in 1993: the Magic picked Chris Webber first overall and immediately swapped him to Golden State for Hardaway and three future first-round picks.

A tall guard who could run an offense and score, he was named to the All-NBA First Team in 1995 and 1996 and was the second star of the 1995 Finals team. After O'Neal left, Hardaway became the face of the club; in the 1997 playoffs against Miami he scored 42 and 41 points in consecutive games. He played six seasons in Orlando and joined the team's Hall of Fame in 2017.
