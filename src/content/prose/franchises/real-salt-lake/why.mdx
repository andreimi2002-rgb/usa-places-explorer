---
title: Why this team matters
---

Real Salt Lake, named in the style of Real Madrid, joined MLS in 2005 and won the <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cup</Term> in 2009, beating the LA Galaxy on penalties. For more than a decade it was led by two club icons, the captain Kyle Beckerman and the goalkeeper Nick Rimando, and in 2011 it reached the final of the CONCACAF Champions League.
