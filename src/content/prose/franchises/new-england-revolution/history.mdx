---

### The founding years (1996–2001)

The Revolution played their first seasons at Foxboro Stadium.

### Five finals (2002–2014)

Under Steve Nicol, coach for a record ten years, and with the striker Taylor Twellman, the club's record scorer, New England became a regular finalist: 2002, 2005, 2006 and 2007, losing each time. It won the U.S. Open Cup in 2007 and the SuperLiga in 2008, and sold the young Clint Dempsey to Fulham. A fifth final followed in 2014.

### Gil and the Shield (2019–today)

The Spanish playmaker Carles Gil arrived in 2019. Under Bruce Arena the Revolution won the 2021 Supporters' Shield, and Gil was named MVP.
