---
title: Biography
---

Grant Hill was already one of the league's best players when Orlando signed him in 2000, after six seasons in Detroit, where he had been Rookie of the Year and a regular All-Star. An ankle injury ruined his Orlando years: across seven seasons he played only a fraction of the team's games. He later rebuilt his career in Phoenix and entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2018.
