### Boston and the early titles (1932–1945)

Founded in Boston as the Braves, the club moved to Washington in 1937 and, with the quarterback Sammy Baugh, won the NFL championship that year and in 1942.

### Joe Gibbs (1981–1992)

Gibbs won Super Bowls XVII, XXII and XXVI with three different quarterbacks. John Riggins, Art Monk and Darrell Green starred.

### A new name (1993–today)

The team moved to Landover, Maryland, in 1997. It dropped the Redskins name in 2020, played as the Washington Football Team and became the Commanders in 2022. Josh Harris's group bought the club in 2023; under Dan Quinn and Daniels, it reached the NFC final in the 2024 season. A new stadium in Washington, on the RFK Stadium site, is planned for 2030.
