---
title: About the city
---

St. Louis has had baseball's Cardinals since 1882; they have won 11 World Series. The Hawks played here from 1955 to 1968 and won the 1958 NBA title with Bob Pettit. Two NFL teams also played here: the Cardinals, from 1960 to 1987, and the Rams, from 1995 to 2015, who won Super Bowl XXXIV. St. Louis City SC joined MLS in 2023. St. Louis runs on Central Time, six hours behind London and eight behind Bucharest for most of the year.
