---
title: Career
---

Phil Jackson was a New York Knicks player from 1967 to 1978. As a coach he was even more successful: he took over the Bulls in 1989 and won six titles with Michael Jordan, then moved to the Lakers and won five more, three with Shaquille O'Neal and Kobe Bryant and two with Bryant and Pau Gasol.

He later returned to the Knicks as team president from 2014 to 2017, and entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> as a coach in 2007.
