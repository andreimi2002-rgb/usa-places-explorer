### The first pick

Nick Anderson, from Chicago, played college basketball at Illinois. In 1989 he became the first player Orlando ever chose in the <Term id="nba-draft">draft</Term>, taken 11th overall for the club's first season.

### Ten seasons (1989–1999)

A strong shooting guard who could defend and score, he grew with the team from the 18-win expansion year to the 1995 Finals and the 60-win season that followed. He played the franchise's first ten seasons before moving to Sacramento in 1999.

### Legacy

Anderson still holds the club records for games played (692) and steals (1,004) and is second in points (10,650). He and Pat Williams were the first two people inducted into the Magic's Hall of Fame, in 2014.
