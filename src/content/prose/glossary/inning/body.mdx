---
title: Inning
---

An inning is one round of play. The visiting team bats first (the "top" of the inning) until the defending team gets three of its batters out; then the home team bats (the "bottom"). A game lasts nine innings and has no time limit; if the score is level after nine, extra innings are played until one team leads at the end of an inning. There are no draws.
