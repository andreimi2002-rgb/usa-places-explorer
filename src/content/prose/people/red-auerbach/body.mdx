---
title: Career
---

Owner Walter Brown hired Red Auerbach as Celtics coach in 1950. He coached Boston to nine titles, including eight in a row from 1959 to 1966, and then left the bench to concentrate on running the club as general manager and later president.

He was part of the Celtics for 57 years, until his death in 2006, and entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> as a coach in 1969.
