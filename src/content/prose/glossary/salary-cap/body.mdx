---
title: Salary cap
---

The salary cap limits how much a team may spend on player wages in a season, so that rich and small markets can compete. The NBA's cap is "soft": a team may go over it, for example to re-sign its own players, but above a second, higher line it pays a luxury tax, and above the "aprons" it loses tools for building its roster.

For 2026-27 the cap is $164.961 million per team. The cap explains many moves that look strange from outside, such as a <Term id="trade">trade</Term> in which salaries must roughly match, or a star leaving in <Term id="free-agency">free agency</Term> because his team cannot afford him.
