---
title: About the city
---

Atlanta has had the Falcons since 1966, the Hawks since 1968, when the team arrived from St. Louis, and Atlanta United since 2017. The Falcons and Atlanta United, both owned by Arthur Blank, share Mercedes-Benz Stadium; the soccer club won the MLS Cup in its second season, 2018. Atlanta runs on Eastern Time, five hours behind London and seven behind Bucharest for most of the year.
