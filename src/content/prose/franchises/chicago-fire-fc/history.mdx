### A champion debut (1998–2005)

The Fire began in 1998 at Soldier Field and won the double straight away, beating D.C. United 2–0 in the MLS Cup final. They added U.S. Open Cups in 2000, 2003 and 2006 and the <Term id="supporters-shield">Supporters' Shield</Term> in 2003.

### Bridgeview (2006–2019)

In 2006 the club moved to its own stadium in suburban Bridgeview. Mexico's Cuauhtémoc Blanco arrived in 2007, Mike Magee was MVP in 2013, and Bastian Schweinsteiger spent his last three seasons here from 2017.

### Back in the city (2020–today)

Joe Mansueto became sole owner in 2019, renamed the club Chicago Fire FC and moved it back to Soldier Field in 2020. Gregg Berhalter, the former US national coach, is in charge in 2026.
