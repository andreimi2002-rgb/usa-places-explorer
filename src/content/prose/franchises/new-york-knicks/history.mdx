### Founding (1946–1964)

The Knicks were founded in 1946 for the first season of the BAA. They reached the Finals in 1951, 1952 and 1953 but lost all three.

### Reed, Frazier and two titles (1964–1974)

Willis Reed, drafted in 1964, and Walt Frazier, drafted in 1967, led the Knicks' first title teams. In 1970 Reed was named league <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> and <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>, and New York won its first title. Reed was Finals MVP again in 1973, when the Knicks won their second.

### Ewing and Riley (1985–2000)

In 1985 the Knicks won the first ever <Term id="draft-lottery">draft lottery</Term> and picked Patrick Ewing, who became the club's all-time leader in points, rebounds, blocks and steals. With Pat Riley as coach from 1991, the Knicks reached the 1994 Finals and lost to Houston in seven games. They reached the Finals again in 1999 and lost to San Antonio.

### Brunson and the 2026 title (2022–today)

Jalen Brunson arrived in 2022, and the Knicks built around him. In 2025 Mike Brown became head coach, and in June 2026 New York beat San Antonio 4–1 in the Finals. Brunson scored 45 points in the deciding game and was named Finals MVP.
