---
title: Home venue
---

The Heat play at the Kaseya Center, which opened in 1999 and hosted its first Heat game on January 2, 2000, a 111–103 win over the Orlando Magic. The building is owned by Miami-Dade County.

It has changed names several times: American Airlines Arena until 2021, then FTX Arena, then briefly Miami-Dade Arena in early 2023 after the FTX deal ended, and Kaseya Center since April 2023.
