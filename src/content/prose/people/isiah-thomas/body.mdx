---
title: Career
---

Detroit drafted Isiah Thomas second overall in 1981, and he played all thirteen seasons of his career with the Pistons. He led them to titles in 1989 and 1990 and was <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term> in 1990. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2000.
