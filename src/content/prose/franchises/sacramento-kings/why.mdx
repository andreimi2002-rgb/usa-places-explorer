---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Kings are one of the oldest franchises in the NBA, and they have played in four cities. They were the Rochester Royals, NBA champions in 1951; then the Cincinnati Royals, with Oscar Robertson; then the Kansas City Kings; and, since 1985, the Sacramento Kings.

Their teams around 2002 were built on Chris Webber and two Serbian players, Vlade Divac and Peja Stojaković.
