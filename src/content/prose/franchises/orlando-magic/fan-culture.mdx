---
title: City and fan culture
---

For a European visitor, the easiest way to understand the Magic is to remember how young the team is. The first season was 1989, so the club's whole history fits inside one lifetime, from the expansion days through the O'Neal and Howard eras to today.

The owners have long described themselves as caretakers rather than owners. The DeVos family, who bought the team in 1991, say the franchise ultimately belongs to Central Florida and its fans. The club also keeps its own <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> for local heroes, which includes stars such as O'Neal, Hardaway, McGrady and Howard, alongside co-founder Pat Williams and early favorite Nick Anderson.

Being in Florida also gives the team a natural rival elsewhere in the state, in Miami. That story has its own page.
