### Road to Orlando

Paolo Banchero was born in Seattle and played one college season at Duke before the 2022 <Term id="nba-draft">draft</Term>, where Orlando took him first overall, the club's fourth No. 1 pick after Shaquille O'Neal, Chris Webber (traded away) and Dwight Howard.

### The centrepiece

A big forward who can handle the ball, pass and score inside and out, he was named Rookie of the Year for 2022-23 and became an <Term id="all-star">All-Star</Term> in 2024. With Franz Wagner and Jalen Suggs he leads the young core that won division titles in 2023-24 and 2024-25. In 2026-27 he begins his fifth season, under the new coach Sean Sweeney.
