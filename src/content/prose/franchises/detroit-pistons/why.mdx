---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Pistons are one of the NBA's oldest clubs, and their name tells the story: the team was founded in 1941 by Fred Zollner, whose factory in Fort Wayne, Indiana, made engine pistons. In 1957 he moved it to Detroit, a much bigger city.

They have won three titles. The teams led by Isiah Thomas and coached by Chuck Daly won in 1989 and 1990, and a team built around Ben Wallace's defense beat the Lakers in 2004.
