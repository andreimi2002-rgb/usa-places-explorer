title: History by eras
---

### Instant success (2017–2018)

Arthur Blank, owner of the NFL's Falcons, founded the club in 2014. Under the Argentine coach Gerardo "Tata" Martino, with Josef Martínez and Miguel Almirón in attack, Atlanta reached the playoffs in its first season and won the MLS Cup in its second, beating Portland 2–0. Martínez was the league MVP.

### After the title (2019–today)

Martino left for the Mexico national team. Atlanta won the U.S. Open Cup and the Campeones Cup in 2019, and sold Almirón to Newcastle and later Thiago Almada for a record fee. Almirón returned as captain in 2025, the goalkeeper Brad Guzan retired after that season, and Martino came back as coach in November 2025.
