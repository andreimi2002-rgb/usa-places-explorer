---
title: Career in Orlando
---

Tracy McGrady spent four seasons in Orlando, from 2000–01 to 2003–04, and in that time he was one of the most dangerous scorers in the world. He led the entire league in points per game twice in a row: 32.1 in 2002–03 and 28.0 in 2003–04.

For European readers, the scoring title is the NBA's equivalent of a golden boot, decided by average rather than total. McGrady was elected to the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2017 and to the Magic's own in 2018.
