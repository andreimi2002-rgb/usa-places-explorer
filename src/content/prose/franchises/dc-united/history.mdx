### The dynasty (1996–2004)

D.C. United played the first MLS season in 1996 at RFK Stadium and won the first MLS Cup, 3–2 after extra time against the LA Galaxy. With Etcheverry, Moreno and the young Ben Olsen they won again in 1997 and 1999, added the <Term id="supporters-shield">Supporters' Shield</Term> in 1997 and 1999, and in 1998 won the CONCACAF Champions' Cup and the Copa Interamericana. A fourth MLS Cup followed in 2004.

### Shields and a long wait (2005–2017)

United won the Supporters' Shield in 2006 and 2007 and the U.S. Open Cup in 2008 and 2013. Dwayne De Rosario was MVP in 2011.

### Audi Field (2018–today)

In July 2018 the club moved into its own stadium, Audi Field, and signed Wayne Rooney, its top scorer in 2018 and 2019. Rooney later returned as head coach from 2022 to 2023. In 2026 the club signed the Romanian striker Louis Munteanu.
