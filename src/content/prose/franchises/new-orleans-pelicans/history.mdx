### The Hornets in New Orleans (2002–2013)

The Hornets moved from Charlotte in 2002. They drafted Chris Paul fourth in 2005, and he and David West led the team. For two seasons, 2005–06 and 2006–07, they played as the New Orleans/Oklahoma City Hornets.

### Anthony Davis and the Pelicans (2012–2019)

Anthony Davis arrived in 2012, and on April 18, 2013 the team became the Pelicans. Gayle Benson took control after her husband, Tom Benson, died in 2018.

### Zion (2019–today)

Zion Williamson arrived in 2019. Coached by Jamahl Mosley, the 2026–27 Pelicans pair him with Trey Murphy III and two young players, Jeremiah Fears and Derik Queen.
