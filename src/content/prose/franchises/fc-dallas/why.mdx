---
title: Why this team matters
---

FC Dallas, founded as the Dallas Burn, is one of MLS's ten original clubs. It has never won the <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cup</Term>, losing the 2010 final, but it won the <Term id="supporters-shield">Supporters' Shield</Term> and the U.S. Open Cup in 2016, and its youth academy is one of the most productive in the country: Weston McKennie and Ricardo Pepi came through it.
