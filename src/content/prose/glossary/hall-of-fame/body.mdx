---
title: Hall of Fame
---

American sports honor their greatest figures with a Hall of Fame: a museum and an honor roll that players, coaches and contributors are voted into after their careers. In basketball it is the Naismith Memorial Basketball Hall of Fame, which covers the whole sport, not just the NBA.

Many teams also keep their own hall of fame for local heroes. The Orlando Magic's started in 2014 and includes O'Neal, Hardaway, McGrady and Howard.
