---
title: About the city
---

New York has had the Yankees since 1903 and the Mets since 1962; the Yankees have won 27 World Series. The Knicks have played here since the NBA's first season, the NFL's Giants since 1925 and the Jets, first called the Titans, since 1960; both football teams play across the river in New Jersey. The Nets moved to Brooklyn in 2012. In soccer, the Red Bulls, a founding MLS club, play in Harrison, New Jersey, and New York City FC joined in 2015. The Dodgers and the baseball Giants also began here before moving to California in 1958. New York City runs on Eastern Time, five hours behind London and seven behind Bucharest for most of the year.
