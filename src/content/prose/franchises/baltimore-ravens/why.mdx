---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Ravens were born in 1996, when the owner Art Modell moved his Cleveland team to Baltimore; the Browns' name and history stayed in Cleveland, so the Ravens started from zero. They have since won two <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowls</Term>, XXXV with a record-setting defence led by Ray Lewis and XLVII under John Harbaugh. Their <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Lamar Jackson was <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> in 2019 and 2023.
