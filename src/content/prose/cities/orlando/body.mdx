---
title: About the city
---

Orlando is the main city of inland Central Florida, with just over 300,000 people at the 2020 census and a much larger metropolitan area around it. Most Europeans know it for its theme parks, but the city also has a busy downtown, where the Magic's arena stands.

For sports, Orlando is a relatively young major-league city: the Magic arrived only in 1989, so the whole of the city's NBA history is recent enough to remember.

Orlando runs on Eastern Time, the same as New York. For most of the year that puts it five hours behind London and seven behind Bucharest (the gap shifts for a few weeks when clocks change), so an evening tip-off in Orlando lands in the middle of the European night. The clock on this page does the maths for your own time zone.
