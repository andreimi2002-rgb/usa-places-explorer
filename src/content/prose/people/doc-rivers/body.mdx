---
title: Biography
---

Doc Rivers played 13 seasons in the NBA, eight of them for Atlanta, before becoming a coach. His first head-coaching job was in Orlando in 1999: with a roster of little-known players, the team went 41–41 and he was named Coach of the Year. He coached the Magic until November 2003, when he was dismissed after a 1–10 start, and went on to a long coaching career elsewhere.
