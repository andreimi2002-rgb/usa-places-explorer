### Milwaukee and St. Louis (1901–1953)

The club played one season as the Milwaukee Brewers in the new American League, then moved to St. Louis as the Browns. It reached only one World Series there, in 1944, and lost it to the Cardinals.

### The Orioles' golden age (1954–1983)

In Baltimore, under the manager Earl Weaver, the Orioles won the World Series in 1966, 1970 and 1983, with the third baseman Brooks Robinson, the outfielder Frank Robinson and the <Term id="pitcher">pitcher</Term> Jim Palmer.

### Camden Yards and Ripken (1984–today)

The club moved into Camden Yards in 1992. On September 6, 1995, Cal Ripken Jr. broke Lou Gehrig's record of consecutive games. David Rubenstein's group bought the club in 2024; the shortstop Gunnar Henderson leads the young team.
