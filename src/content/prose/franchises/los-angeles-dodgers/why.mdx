---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Dodgers changed baseball in 1947, when they brought in Jackie Robinson, the first Black player in the modern major leagues; every team now wears his number 42 on April 15. Founded in Brooklyn, they moved to Los Angeles in 1958 and have won nine <Term id="world-series">World Series</Term>, including 2024 and 2025, the first repeat champions in 25 years. Their star is the Japanese two-way player Shohei Ohtani.
