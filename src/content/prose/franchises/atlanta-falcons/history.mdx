### The early years (1966–2007)

The Falcons began in 1966 as an expansion team. The linebacker Tommy Nobis, their first draft pick, and the cornerback Deion Sanders were early stars. They reached Super Bowl XXXIII in the 1998 season. Arthur Blank bought the club in 2002.

### Matt Ryan (2008–2021)

Matt Ryan, drafted in 2008, was MVP in 2016 and, with Julio Jones, led the team to Super Bowl LI.

### Today (2022–today)

The Falcons moved into Mercedes-Benz Stadium in 2017. In 2026 they hired Kevin Stefanski and signed the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Tua Tagovailoa, joining the running back Bijan Robinson, a First-team All-Pro in 2025.
