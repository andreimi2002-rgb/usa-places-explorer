### Beginnings (1980–1998)

Dallas joined the NBA as an expansion team in 1980. Mark Aguirre, Rolando Blackman and Derek Harper led the teams of the 1980s.

### Dirk and the 2011 title (1998–2019)

Milwaukee drafted Dirk Nowitzki ninth in 1998 and he went straight to Dallas, where he stayed for 21 seasons. Mark Cuban bought the team in January 2000, and the Mavericks moved into American Airlines Center in 2001. They lost the 2006 Finals to Miami; Nowitzki was <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> in 2007, and in 2011 Dallas beat Miami in a rematch for its first title, with Nowitzki as <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term> and Jason Kidd at point guard.

### Luka (2018–2025)

Luka Dončić arrived in 2018 and was Rookie of the Year. The Mavericks reached the 2024 Finals and lost to Boston. In late 2023 the Adelson and Dumont families bought control of the club from Cuban, who kept a minority stake.

### Flagg (2025–today)

Cooper Flagg joined in 2025. Dusty May replaced Jason Kidd as coach in June 2026.
