### Chicago and Baltimore (1961–1973)

The franchise was admitted in 1961 as the Chicago Packers and was renamed the Zephyrs a year later. In 1963 it moved to Baltimore as the Bullets. Wes Unseld, drafted second in 1968, was <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> and Rookie of the Year in the same season, and Earl Monroe starred alongside him.

### The Washington Bullets (1973–1997)

The team moved to the Washington area in 1973, first as the Capital Bullets. With Unseld and Elvin Hayes it reached the Finals in 1975, 1978 and 1979, and won the 1978 title against Seattle.

### The Wizards (1997–today)

On May 15, 1997, the Bullets became the Wizards and moved into today's Capital One Arena. Michael Jordan played two seasons for them, Gilbert Arenas, John Wall and Bradley Beal led later teams, and Ted Leonsis completed his takeover in 2010. Coached by Brian Keefe, the 2026–27 Wizards combine veterans Trae Young and Anthony Davis with AJ Dybantsa, the first pick of 2026.
