---
title: Career
---

Chicago drafted Michael Jordan third overall in 1984, a 6-foot-6 guard from North Carolina. He led the league in scoring ten times and was named <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> five times.

With Scottie Pippen and coach Phil Jackson he won three titles in a row from 1991 to 1993, retired, tried professional baseball, came back, and won three more from 1996 to 1998. He was <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term> all six times. He later played two seasons for Washington, and entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2009.
