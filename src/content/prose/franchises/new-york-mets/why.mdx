---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Mets were created in 1962 to give New York a National League team again, after the Dodgers and Giants left for California. Terrible at first, they became the "Miracle Mets" of 1969, winning the <Term id="world-series">World Series</Term> seven years after their creation, and won again in 1986. Their owner, Steve Cohen, has made them one of baseball's biggest spenders.
