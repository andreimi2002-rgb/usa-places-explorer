---
title: Career
---

Boston drafted Larry Bird sixth overall in 1978, and he joined the team for the 1979–80 season, when he was named Rookie of the Year. He played all thirteen seasons of his career with the Celtics.

He won titles in 1981, 1984 and 1986, was <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term> in 1984 and 1986, and was league <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> three years in a row. His battles with Magic Johnson's Lakers defined the 1980s. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 1998.
