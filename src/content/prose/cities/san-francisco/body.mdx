---
title: About the city
---

San Francisco has had the 49ers since 1946 and baseball's Giants since 1958, when they arrived from New York; the 49ers have played in Santa Clara, in Silicon Valley, since 2014. The Warriors played here from 1962 to 1971, spent almost fifty years in Oakland and came back in 2019, into Chase Center. San Francisco runs on Pacific Time, eight hours behind London and ten behind Bucharest for most of the year.
