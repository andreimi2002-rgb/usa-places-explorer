---
title: Fans and game day
---

The Magic are Central Florida's own team in a region full of visitors, and the owners have long described themselves as caretakers: the DeVos family says the franchise ultimately belongs to Central Florida and its fans. Because the club is young, its whole history, from the expansion days through the O'Neal and Howard eras, fits inside one lifetime, and many fans in the arena remember all of it.

Home games are at the Kia Center in downtown Orlando, a short walk from the Church Street station of SunRail, the region's commuter train. Through the "Train to the Game" programme, Magic ticket holders can ride SunRail free to and from weekday home games. Inside, the night is run by Stuff the Magic Dragon, the mascot since 1989.

The natural rival is in the same state: the Miami Heat, who joined the league a year earlier. That story has its own page.
