---
title: Why this team matters
---

The 76ers' stars have included Dolph Schayes, Wilt Chamberlain, Julius Erving, Moses Malone, Allen Iverson and Joel Embiid. They have won three titles, in 1955 as the Syracuse Nationals and in 1967 and 1983 in Philadelphia.

In 2026 they traded for Jaylen Brown and signing LeBron James to play alongside Embiid and Tyrese Maxey.
