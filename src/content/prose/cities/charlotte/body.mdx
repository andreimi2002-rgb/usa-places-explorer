---
title: About the city
---

Charlotte has had the NFL's Panthers since 1995. It had the original Hornets from 1988 to 2002, and the Bobcats, today's Hornets, since 2004. Charlotte FC joined MLS in 2022 and shares Bank of America Stadium with the Panthers; both are owned by David Tepper. Charlotte runs on Eastern Time, five hours behind London and seven behind Bucharest for most of the year.
