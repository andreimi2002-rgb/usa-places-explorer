### Paul Brown's club (1968–1991)

Paul Brown founded the Bengals in 1967; they began play in the AFL in 1968 and joined the NFL in 1970. With Ken Anderson, MVP in 1981, they reached Super Bowl XVI, and with Boomer Esiason, MVP in 1988, Super Bowl XXIII. Brown's son Mike has run the club since his death in 1991.

### Burrow (2020–today)

Joe Burrow was the first pick of the 2020 <Term id="nfl-draft">draft</Term>, and his LSU team-mate Ja'Marr Chase joined in 2021. Under Zac Taylor, coach since 2019, they reached Super Bowl LVI in the 2021 season. The Bengals went 6–11 in 2025, with Burrow injured for much of the year.
