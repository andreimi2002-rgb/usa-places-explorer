---
title: Super Bowl
---

The Super Bowl is the NFL's final: one game, on a neutral ground chosen years in advance, between the champions of the two conferences. The first was played in January 1967, after the 1966 season, between the NFL's Green Bay Packers and the AFL's Kansas City Chiefs. Games are numbered in Roman numerals: Super Bowl LX, won by the Seattle Seahawks in February 2026, was the 60th. The half-time show and the television adverts make it a national event far beyond the sport.
