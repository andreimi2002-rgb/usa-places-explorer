### Landry's Cowboys (1960–1988)

Founded in 1960 with Tom Landry as coach, the only one for 29 seasons, Dallas played at the Cotton Bowl and from 1971 at Texas Stadium. With Roger Staubach it won Super Bowls VI and XII.

### The 1990s dynasty (1989–2008)

Jerry Jones bought the club in 1989 and hired Jimmy Johnson. Aikman, Smith and Irvin won Super Bowls XXVII, XXVIII and XXX; Emmitt Smith later became the NFL's all-time leading rusher.

### Arlington (2009–today)

In 2009 the club moved into AT&T Stadium in Arlington. Dak Prescott, Rookie of the Year in 2016, has been the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> since; the Cowboys have not reached a Super Bowl since 1995.
