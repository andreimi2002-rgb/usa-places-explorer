---
title: Career
---

Lenny Wilkens joined the SuperSonics in 1968 as a player and soon became their <Term id="player-coach">player-coach</Term>. He returned to coach Seattle from 1977 to 1985 and led the team to its only title, in 1979.

He is in the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> twice: as a player (1989) and as a coach (1998).
