---
title: Career
---

Seattle picked Scottie Pippen fifth in the 1987 draft, and the Bulls traded for him the same night. Alongside Michael Jordan he won all six of Chicago's titles, in 1991–93 and 1996–98.

He later played for Houston and Portland, returned to Chicago for one final season in 2003–04, and entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2010.
