### Halas's Bears (1920–1967)

Founded in 1920 as the Decatur Staleys, the club moved to Chicago in 1921 and became the Bears in 1922. Halas coached in four spells, a <Term id="player-coach">player-coach</Term> at first, and the "Monsters of the Midway" won eight NFL championships, the last in 1963.

### Payton and the 1985 Bears (1968–1992)

The Bears moved into Soldier Field in 1971. Walter Payton ran for them from 1975 to 1987. Under Mike Ditka the 1985 team lost one game all season and beat New England 46–10 in Super Bowl XX.

### After Ditka (1993–today)

In the 2006 season the Bears reached Super Bowl XLI and lost to Indianapolis. They drafted the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Caleb Williams first overall in 2024 and hired Ben Johnson in 2025. In his first season Chicago went 11–6, won the NFC North and a playoff game against Green Bay.
