---
title: Career
---

San Antonio drafted Tim Duncan first overall in 1997, and he played all nineteen seasons of his career with the Spurs. He was Rookie of the Year in 1998.

He won five titles, in 1999, 2003, 2005, 2007 and 2014, and was <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term> three times. He was league <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> in 2002 and 2003, and entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2020.
