title: Career
---

LeBron James was drafted first overall by Cleveland in 2003. In 2010 he joined Miami, and with Dwyane Wade and Chris Bosh he reached four straight Finals, winning the titles of 2012 and 2013 and the <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term> award both times.

He returned to Cleveland and won there in 2016, then joined the Lakers as a <Term id="free-agency">free agent</Term> in 2018 and won the 2020 title against Miami. He has been named league <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> four times. In 2026–27, his 24th season, he plays for the Philadelphia 76ers.
