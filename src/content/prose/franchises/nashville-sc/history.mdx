### A new club (2016–2021)

Founded in 2016 and awarded an MLS place in 2017, Nashville played its first MLS match on February 29, 2020, losing 2–1 to Atlanta at the NFL's Nissan Stadium; Walker Zimmerman scored the first goal.

### Geodis Park and trophies (2022–today)

The club moved into its own stadium in 2022, the year Mukhtar won the MVP award and the Golden Boot. Nashville lost the 2023 Leagues Cup final to Inter Miami on penalties, won the 2025 U.S. Open Cup against Austin and, in 2026, the Supporters' Shield for the best regular season.
