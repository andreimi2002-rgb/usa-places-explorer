---
title: Why this team matters
---

New York City FC is the club of City Football Group, the owners of Manchester City, together with the owners of the New York Yankees. It began in 2015 with three European stars, David Villa, Frank Lampard and Andrea Pirlo, and won the <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cup</Term> in 2021. It still plays in a baseball stadium, Yankee Stadium, while its own ground is built.
