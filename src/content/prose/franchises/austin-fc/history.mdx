### A new club (2018–2021)

After the Columbus fans kept the Crew, Precourt was given an expansion club in Austin, founded on October 12, 2018. It played its first match on April 17, 2021, a 2–0 loss at LAFC, and opened Q2 Stadium that June.

### Today (2022–today)

With the Argentine Sebastián Driussi, its first All-Star, Austin reached the 2022 Western Conference final. It lost the 2025 U.S. Open Cup final to Nashville. Jim Curtin, formerly of the Philadelphia Union, has been named to take over as coach after the 2026 season.
