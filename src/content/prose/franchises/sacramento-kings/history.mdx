title: History by eras
---

### Rochester (1948–1957)

The Rochester Royals joined the BAA in 1948 and won the 1951 NBA title in Game 7 against New York, with Bob Davies among their stars.

### Cincinnati (1957–1972)

The team moved to Cincinnati in 1957. Oscar Robertson played ten seasons there, and was Rookie of the Year in 1961 and MVP in 1964.

### Kansas City (1972–1985)

In 1972 the franchise moved to Kansas City as the Kansas City-Omaha Kings, with Nate "Tiny" Archibald as its star, and later became simply the Kansas City Kings.

### Sacramento (1985–today)

The Kings moved to Sacramento for the 1985–86 season. Chris Webber, Vlade Divac and Peja Stojaković arrived in 1998. Vivek Ranadivé's group bought the team in 2013 and opened Golden 1 Center in 2016. Coached by Doug Christie, the 2026–27 Kings hand the offense to rookie Darius Acuff Jr., the seventh pick of 2026.
