---
title: About the city
---

Chicago has had the Cubs since the National League's first season, 1876; they have played at Wrigley Field since 1916. The Bears arrived in 1921, a year after they were founded in Decatur; the Bulls began in 1966 and the Fire in 1998, winning the MLS Cup in its first season. The NFL's Cardinals, today in Arizona, also began here and stayed until 1959. Chicago runs on Central Time, six hours behind London and eight behind Bucharest for most of the year.
