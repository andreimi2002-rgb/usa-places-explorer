---
title: Why this team matters
---

For many Europeans, the Chicago Bulls were the first NBA team they ever heard of. In the 1990s, with Michael Jordan, Scottie Pippen and coach Phil Jackson, they won six titles in eight seasons, and in 1995–96 they won 72 of 82 regular-season games.

The club itself is younger than you might think. It joined the NBA as an <Term id="expansion-team">expansion team</Term> in 1966, and its first season, 33 wins and 48 losses, was the best ever by a new club at the time.
