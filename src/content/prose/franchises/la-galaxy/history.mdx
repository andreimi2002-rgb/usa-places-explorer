### The founding years (1996–2006)

The Galaxy played MLS's first season in 1996 at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena and lost three of the first six finals, in 1996, 1999 and 2001. The first title came in 2002, the second in 2005, and in 2000 the club won the CONCACAF Champions' Cup. In 2003 it moved into its own stadium in Carson.

### Beckham and Donovan (2007–2016)

David Beckham's arrival from Real Madrid in 2007 made the Galaxy world-famous; the <Term id="designated-player">Designated Player</Term> rule was created for him. With Landon Donovan and, from 2011, Robbie Keane, the team won the MLS Cup in 2011, 2012 and 2014.

### Zlatan and a sixth title (2017–today)

Zlatan Ibrahimović scored 30 goals in 2019. The Galaxy won a record sixth MLS Cup in 2024, beating the New York Red Bulls 2–1.
