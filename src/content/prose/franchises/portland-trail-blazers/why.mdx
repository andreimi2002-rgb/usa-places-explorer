---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Trail Blazers joined the NBA in 1970 and won the title in only their seventh season, in 1977, with Bill Walton as <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>. They reached the Finals again in 1990 and 1992 with Clyde Drexler.

In the 2010s and again in 2026–27 they were Damian Lillard's team, and in 2026 a new owner, Tom Dundon, bought the club from the estate of Paul Allen.
