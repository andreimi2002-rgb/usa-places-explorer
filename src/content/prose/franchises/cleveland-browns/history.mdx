### Paul Brown and Otto Graham (1946–1962)

The Browns began in 1946 in the All-America Football Conference and won all four of its titles. In the NFL from 1950 they won the championship in 1950, 1954 and 1955, with Otto Graham at <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term>.

### Jim Brown (1957–1965)

Jim Brown, three times MVP, led the Browns to the 1964 NFL title, still their last.

### The move and the return (1966–1999)

Bernie Kosar's teams of the 1980s lost three AFC finals. In 1996 the owner Art Modell took the players to Baltimore, where they became the Ravens. The NFL kept the Browns' name and records in Cleveland, and the team came back in 1999 in a new stadium.

### Today (2000–today)

Jimmy and Dee Haslam bought the club in 2012. In June 2026 the Browns traded Myles Garrett, twice Defensive Player of the Year, to the Rams, and Todd Monken became coach.
