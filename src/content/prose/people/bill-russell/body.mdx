title: Career
---

Bill Russell came to Boston in 1956 through a <Term id="trade">trade</Term> with St. Louis, and in thirteen seasons he won eleven titles. He was league <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> five times.

When Red Auerbach stepped down as coach in 1966, Russell became <Term id="player-coach">player-coach</Term> and the NBA's first Black head coach, and he won the 1968 and 1969 titles in that double role. Since 2009 the <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term> award carries his name. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> as a player in 1975 and as a coach in 2021, and died on July 31, 2022.
