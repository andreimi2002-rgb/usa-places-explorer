---
title: Draft lottery
---

If the worst team were simply guaranteed the first pick, teams would have a reason to lose on purpose. The draft lottery is the NBA's answer: it is a drawing among the teams that missed the playoffs, with the weakest records getting the best odds but no certainty.

The details are changed from time to time. The league approved a new version in 2026, first used for the 2027 draft, that adds more teams to the drawing and flattens the odds further. The principle stays the same: bad seasons improve your chances, but they do not buy you the top pick.
