---
title: Player-coach
---

A player-coach plays for a team and is its head coach at the same time.

The best-known example is Bill Russell, who took over as Boston's coach in 1966 while still playing and won the 1968 and 1969 titles. He was also the league's first Black head coach.

In the early NFL it was common: George Halas played for the Bears and coached them through the 1920s, as well as owning the club.
