### The AFL and Shula (1966–1982)

Founded in 1965, the Dolphins began play in the AFL in 1966. Don Shula arrived in 1970. Miami lost Super Bowl VI, then won VII and VIII, with Bob Griese at <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> and Larry Csonka running the ball.

### Marino (1983–1999)

Dan Marino, drafted in 1983, was MVP in 1984 and led Miami to Super Bowl XIX, lost to San Francisco. He played 17 seasons without winning the title. The club moved into today's stadium in 1987.

### Today (2009–today)

Stephen Ross has owned the club since 2009. In 2026 the Dolphins started again under the coach Jeff Hafley, after letting the quarterback Tua Tagovailoa go and trading the receiver Jaylen Waddle to Denver.
