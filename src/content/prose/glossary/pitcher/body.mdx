---
title: Pitcher
---

The pitcher stands on a small mound in the middle of the infield and throws the ball towards the batter. Each pitch is a duel: a pitch in the strike zone that the batter misses or does not swing at is a strike, three strikes and the batter is out; four pitches outside the zone (balls) and the batter walks to first base. Starting pitchers usually pitch every fifth day; relief pitchers, or "closers", finish games. The best pitcher in each league wins the Cy Young Award.
