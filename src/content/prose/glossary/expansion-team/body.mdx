---
title: Expansion team
---

North American leagues grow by adding brand-new teams, called expansion teams, instead of promoting clubs from a lower division. There is no relegation or promotion in the NBA: a city gets a team because the league decides to grant one, usually in return for a large fee.

New teams are usually built from players other clubs make available, so their first seasons are often hard. In the late 1980s the NBA added four expansion teams in two years: Charlotte and Miami in 1988, Minnesota and Orlando in 1989.
