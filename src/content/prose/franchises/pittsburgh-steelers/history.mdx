### The early years (1933–1968)

Art Rooney founded the club on July 8, 1933, as the Pittsburgh Pirates; it became the Steelers in 1940. It played at Forbes Field and Pitt Stadium without a title.

### The Steel Curtain (1969–1991)

Chuck Noll arrived in 1969. With Terry Bradshaw, Franco Harris and a defence led by Joe Greene and Jack Lambert, the Steelers won Super Bowls IX, X, XIII and XIV.

### Cowher and Tomlin (1992–2025)

Bill Cowher and then Mike Tomlin, with Ben Roethlisberger at <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term>, won Super Bowls XL and XLIII. The team moved into today's stadium in 2001.

### A new era (2026–today)

Tomlin stepped down after the 2025 season, and Mike McCarthy was hired in January 2026. Aaron Rodgers and T. J. Watt, the 2021 Defensive Player of the Year, lead the team.
