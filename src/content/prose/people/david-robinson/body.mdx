---
title: Career
---

The Spurs drafted David Robinson first overall in 1987, and he joined them in 1989, winning Rookie of the Year. He played his whole career in San Antonio.

He was Defensive Player of the Year in 1992 and league <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> in 1995, and won the 1999 and 2003 titles alongside Tim Duncan. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2009.
