title: Why this team matters
---

Denver is the "Mile High City", about 1,600 m above sea level, and the thin air is part of the Broncos' home advantage. They have reached eight <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowls</Term> and won three: XXXII and XXXIII with the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> John Elway, and 50 with Peyton Manning.
