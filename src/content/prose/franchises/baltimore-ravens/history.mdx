### A new team (1996–2007)

The Ravens began in 1996 and moved into today's stadium in 1998. Built around Ray Lewis and the tackle Jonathan Ogden, their first draft pick, they won Super Bowl XXXV over the New York Giants.

### Harbaugh (2008–2025)

John Harbaugh coached the team for 18 seasons. With Joe Flacco at quarterback they beat his brother Jim's San Francisco 49ers in Super Bowl XLVII. Lamar Jackson was drafted in 2018 and won two MVP awards.

### Today (2026–today)

After an 8–9 season, Harbaugh left for the Giants, and Jesse Minter became the fourth head coach in club history in January 2026.
