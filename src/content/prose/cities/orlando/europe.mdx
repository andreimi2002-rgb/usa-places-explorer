---
title: For European fans
---

Orlando runs on Eastern Time, the same as New York. For most of the year that puts it five hours behind London and seven behind Bucharest (the gap shifts for a few weeks in spring and autumn, because Europe and the United States change their clocks on different dates), so an evening tip-off lands in the middle of the European night. The clock on this page does the maths for your own time zone.

If you are combining a game with a holiday, the NBA season, from October to April, avoids the hottest and wettest months. Weekday games are the easiest to reach by train from the northern and southern suburbs, thanks to free SunRail rides for ticket holders.
