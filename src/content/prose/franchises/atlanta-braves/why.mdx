---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Braves are the oldest continuously operating club in American professional sport, founded in Boston in 1871. They moved to Milwaukee in 1953 and to Atlanta in 1966. Hank Aaron broke Babe Ruth's <Term id="home-run">home run</Term> record for them in 1974, and they have won four <Term id="world-series">World Series</Term>, in Boston, Milwaukee and twice in Atlanta.
