### Tri-Cities and Milwaukee (1949–1955)

The franchise played its first NBA season in 1949–50 as the Tri-Cities Blackhawks, moved to Milwaukee in 1951 and drafted Bob Pettit there in 1954.

### St. Louis and the 1958 title (1955–1968)

In St. Louis Pettit became the league's first MVP in 1956. In 1958 the Hawks beat Boston for the title, with Cliff Hagan, who had come from Boston in the 1956 Bill Russell trade, beside him. They met Boston again in the Finals in 1957, 1960 and 1961 and lost each time.

### Atlanta (1968–today)

Tom Cousins and Carl Sanders bought the team and moved it to Atlanta in 1968. Dominique Wilkins arrived in 1982 and led the league in scoring in 1986. Lenny Wilkens coached the team from 1993 to 2000, and the Hawks moved into today's State Farm Arena in 1999. Trae Young, who arrived in 2018, was traded to Washington in 2026. Coached by Quin Snyder, the 2026–27 Hawks are built around Jalen Johnson.
