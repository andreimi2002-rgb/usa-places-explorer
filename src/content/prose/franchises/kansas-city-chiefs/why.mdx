---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Chiefs are the dominant team of the 2020s. With the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Patrick Mahomes and the coach Andy Reid they won three <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowls</Term> in five seasons, LIV, LVII and LVIII. The club was founded by Lamar Hunt as the Dallas Texans, one of the original AFL teams, and played in the very first Super Bowl.
