---
title: Biography
---

Stan Van Gundy coached the Magic for five seasons, from 2007 to 2012. Around Dwight Howard he built a team of shooters, and Orlando won 52, 59 and 59 games in his first three seasons. In 2009 his team beat Philadelphia, Boston and Cleveland to reach the NBA Finals, lost 4–1 to the Lakers, and in 2010 it reached the conference finals again. His Orlando record was 259–135 in the regular season and 31–28 in the <Term id="playoffs">playoffs</Term>, the most playoff wins of any Magic coach. He left in 2012, the summer Howard was traded.
