---
title: City and fan culture
---

The Warriors have had four homes: Philadelphia until 1962, San Francisco until 1971, Oakland until 2019, and San Francisco again since then. The name "Golden State" came with the move to Oakland in 1971.

The Bay Area runs on Pacific Time, eight hours behind London and ten behind Bucharest for most of the year, so Warriors home games usually start in the European early morning.
