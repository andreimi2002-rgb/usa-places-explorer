title: History by eras
---

### Early years (1968–1988)

Phoenix joined the NBA as an expansion team in 1968. With Alvan Adams and Paul Westphal, the Suns reached the 1976 Finals and lost to Boston.

### Kevin Johnson and Barkley (1988–1996)

Kevin Johnson arrived in 1988, and Charles Barkley joined in 1992. In 1993 they reached the Finals and lost to Chicago.

### Nash (2004–2012)

Steve Nash, whom Phoenix had drafted in 1996, returned in 2004 and was MVP in 2005 and 2006, with Amar'e Stoudemire and Shawn Marion.

### Booker and the 2021 Finals (2015–today)

Devin Booker arrived in 2015. With Chris Paul, the Suns reached the 2021 Finals and lost to Milwaukee. Mat Ishbia bought the team in 2023, and in 2025 Phoenix traded Kevin Durant to Houston for Dillon Brooks and Jalen Green.
