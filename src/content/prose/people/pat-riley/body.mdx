---
title: Career
---

Pat Riley played five seasons for the Lakers in the 1970s and became their head coach in November 1981. In nine seasons on the bench he won four titles with the Showtime teams.

After four seasons coaching the New York Knicks, he moved to Miami in September 1995 as coach and president. He coached the Heat to their first title in 2006, and as president he built the teams that won in 2012 and 2013. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2008.
