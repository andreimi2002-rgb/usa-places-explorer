---
title: About the arena
---

The Kia Center sits in downtown Orlando and has been the Magic's home since it opened on October 1, 2010. For its first thirteen years it carried the name of Amway, the company co-founded by the late Magic owner Rich DeVos. In December 2023 a naming deal with the carmaker Kia gave it its current name.

It is a modern indoor arena built for basketball first, but like most American arenas it is busy far beyond the NBA season, with concerts, ice hockey and other events filling the calendar.

If you visit from Europe, think of it less like a football stadium and more like a large concert hall with a court in the middle: seats rise steeply on all sides, and the game is only part of the show.
