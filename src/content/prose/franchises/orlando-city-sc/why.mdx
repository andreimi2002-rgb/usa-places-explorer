---
title: Why this team matters
---

Orlando City is the city's soccer club, the Lions, and one of the most visible examples of how MLS has grown. Founded in a lower league in 2010, it joined MLS in 2015 and opened its own stadium two years later.

The club has signed stars from the start: Kaká was its first <Term id="designated-player">Designated Player</Term>, Nani followed in 2019 and Antoine Griezmann arrived in 2026. It won its first major trophy, the U.S. Open Cup, in 2022 and is still chasing a first <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cup</Term>.
