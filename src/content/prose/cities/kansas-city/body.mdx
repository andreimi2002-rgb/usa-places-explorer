---
title: About the city
---

Kansas City, Missouri, has had the Chiefs since 1963, when they arrived from Dallas, and they have played at Arrowhead Stadium since 1972. Sporting Kansas City, a founding MLS club, plays across the state line in Kansas City, Kansas. The NBA's Kings played here from 1972 to 1985. Kansas City runs on Central Time, six hours behind London and eight behind Bucharest for most of the year.
