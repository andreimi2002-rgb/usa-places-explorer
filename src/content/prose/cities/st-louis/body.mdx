---
title: About the city
---

St. Louis had the Hawks from 1955 to 1968, and they won the 1958 title here with Bob Pettit. The NFL's Rams played here from 1995 to 2015 and won Super Bowl XXXIV. St. Louis City SC joined MLS in 2023. St. Louis runs on Central Time, six hours behind London and eight behind Bucharest for most of the year.
