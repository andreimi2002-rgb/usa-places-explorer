### A record start (2022–2023)

Charlotte played its first match on February 26, 2022, a 3–0 loss at D.C. United, and its first home game a week later in front of 74,479. The Polish striker Karol Świderski, the first Designated Player, was the top league scorer in 2022 and 2023.

### Dean Smith (2024–today)

The English coach Dean Smith took over in December 2023, and the English midfielder Ashley Westwood is captain.
