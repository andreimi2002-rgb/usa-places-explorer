### New York (1883–1957)

Founded in 1883, the New York Giants won World Series titles in 1905, 1921, 1922, 1933 and 1954, the last with Willie Mays.

### San Francisco (1958–2009)

The Giants moved west with the Dodgers in 1958. Mays, Willie McCovey and Juan Marichal starred, and later Barry Bonds, MVP seven times. The team moved into today's ballpark in 2000.

### The even years (2010–today)

With the catcher Buster Posey and the <Term id="pitcher">pitcher</Term> Madison Bumgarner, the Giants won the World Series in 2010, 2012 and 2014. Posey now runs the club's baseball operations; it traded for Rafael Devers in 2025 and hired Tony Vitello as manager.
