---
title: Why this team matters
---

D.C. United were MLS's first great team. Coached by Bruce Arena, they won three of the first four <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cups</Term>, in 1996, 1997 and 1999, and a fourth in 2004. In 1998 they became the first MLS club to win the CONCACAF Champions' Cup. The Bolivians Marco Etcheverry and Jaime Moreno were the stars of those years, and Wayne Rooney played here in 2018 and 2019.
