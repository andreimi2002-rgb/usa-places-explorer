### Chicago (1898–1959)

The club began as an amateur team in Chicago in 1898 and was one of the NFL's founders in 1920. With the halfback Charley Trippi it won the 1947 championship.

### St. Louis and Arizona (1960–2011)

The Cardinals moved to St. Louis in 1960 and to the Phoenix area in 1988. The safety Pat Tillman left the team in 2002 to join the US Army and was killed in Afghanistan in 2004. In the 2008 season the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Kurt Warner and Larry Fitzgerald took them to Super Bowl XLIII.

### Today (2012–today)

After a 3–14 season in 2025, the Cardinals hired Mike LaFleur. The tight end Trey McBride was a First-team All-Pro in 2025.
