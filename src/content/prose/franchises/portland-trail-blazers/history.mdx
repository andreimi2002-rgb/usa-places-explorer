title: History by eras
---

### The 1977 title (1970–1978)

Portland joined the NBA in 1970 and drafted Bill Walton first overall in 1974. In 1977, with Maurice Lucas beside him, Walton led the Blazers past Philadelphia for their only title.

### Drexler (1983–1995)

Clyde Drexler, with Terry Porter and Jerome Kersey, took Portland to the Finals in 1990, lost to Detroit, and in 1992, lost to Chicago.

### Lillard (2012–2023)

Portland drafted Damian Lillard sixth overall in 2012, and he was Rookie of the Year. He spent eleven seasons with the club before moving to Milwaukee in 2023.

### A new start (2026–today)

In 2026 Tom Dundon's group bought the team, Micah Nori became coach, and Lillard came back to play alongside Ja Morant and Deni Avdija.
