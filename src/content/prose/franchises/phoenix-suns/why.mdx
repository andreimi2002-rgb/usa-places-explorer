---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Suns joined the NBA in 1968 and have reached the Finals three times, in 1976, 1993 and 2021, without winning. Their stars have included Charles Barkley, who led the 1993 team, and Steve Nash, who was named <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> two years running.

Today they are Devin Booker's team.
