### Denver years (1996–2006)

The Rapids played their first eleven seasons at Denver's NFL stadiums, first Mile High Stadium and then its successor. Marcelo Balboa scored the club's first goal, and the team reached the 1997 final.

### Commerce City (2007–today)

In 2007 the club moved into its own stadium in Commerce City. With Pablo Mastroeni in midfield and Conor Casey up front, it won the 2010 MLS Cup. Mastroeni later coached the team, and Tim Howard kept goal from 2016.
