---
title: Career
---

Portland drafted Bill Walton first overall in 1974, and in 1977 he led the Blazers to their only title as <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>. He was league <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> the following season. He later won a second title with Boston in 1986, entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 1993 and died on May 27, 2024.
