---
title: Why this rivalry matters
---

From 2015 to 2018 Golden State and Cleveland met in the <Term id="nba-finals">NBA Finals</Term> four years in a row.

### Four Finals

Golden State won in 2015. In 2016 the Warriors, coming off a record 73-win season, lost to LeBron James's Cavaliers in seven games. Kevin Durant then joined Golden State, and the Warriors won in 2017 and swept Cleveland 4–0 in 2018.

### Why it matters

For four seasons the NBA had a fixed final, and two of the defining players of the era, Stephen Curry and LeBron James, met at the end of every season.
