### Arrival (2009–2015)

Seattle joined MLS in 2009 and won its first home match 3–0 against the New York Red Bulls. The club won the U.S. Open Cup in each of its first three seasons and the <Term id="supporters-shield">Supporters' Shield</Term> in 2014, with Clint Dempsey and Obafemi Martins in attack.

### Champions (2016–today)

In July 2016 Brian Schmetzer took over as coach and Nicolás Lodeiro arrived. That year Seattle won its first MLS Cup, on penalties against Toronto, with goalkeeper Stefan Frei as MVP. It beat Toronto again in the 2019 final, won the CONCACAF Champions League in 2022 and the Leagues Cup in 2025.
