---
title: About the city
---

Atlanta has had the Braves and the Falcons since 1966; the Braves arrived from Milwaukee and have won two of their four World Series here. The Hawks arrived from St. Louis in 1968, and Atlanta United began in 2017; the Falcons and Atlanta United, both owned by Arthur Blank, share Mercedes-Benz Stadium. Atlanta runs on Eastern Time, five hours behind London and seven behind Bucharest for most of the year.
