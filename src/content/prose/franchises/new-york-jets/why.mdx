---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Jets won one of the most famous games in American football: <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowl</Term> III, in January 1969. Their <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Joe Namath guaranteed a win over the favourites, the NFL's Baltimore Colts, and delivered it, 16–7. It was the first Super Bowl won by an AFL team, and the Jets have not been back since.
