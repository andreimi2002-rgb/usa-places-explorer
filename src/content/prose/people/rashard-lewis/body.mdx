---
title: Biography
---

Rashard Lewis went straight from high school to the 1998 draft and spent nine seasons in Seattle before Orlando signed him in 2007. A tall forward who shot from distance, he was a key part of the Van Gundy teams and an All-Star in 2009, the season the Magic reached the Finals. He was traded to Washington in 2010 and later won a title with Miami in 2013.
