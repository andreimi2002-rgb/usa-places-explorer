### Expansion (1976–1995)

The Buccaneers began in 1976 and lost all 14 games that season. The defensive end Lee Roy Selmon, their first draft pick, became their first Hall of Famer. The Glazer family bought the club in 1995.

### The great defence (1996–2008)

Warren Sapp, Derrick Brooks, John Lynch and Ronde Barber formed one of the best defences of the era, and under Jon Gruden the Buccaneers won Super Bowl XXXVII.

### Brady (2020–today)

Tom Brady joined in 2020 and won Super Bowl LV at Raymond James Stadium. Todd Bowles became coach in 2022. The team won four straight division titles from 2021 to 2024, the last two with the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Baker Mayfield.
