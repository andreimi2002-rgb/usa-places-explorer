### The early titles (1925–1978)

Tim Mara founded the club in 1925. The Giants won the NFL championship in 1927, 1934, 1938 and 1956, playing at the Polo Grounds and Yankee Stadium, and moved to New Jersey in 1976.

### Parcells and Taylor (1979–1993)

Bill Parcells, coach from 1983 to 1990, with the linebacker Lawrence Taylor and the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Phil Simms, won Super Bowls XXI and XXV.

### Eli Manning (2004–2019)

Eli Manning led the Giants to Super Bowls XLII and XLVI, both against Tom Brady's Patriots, and was MVP of both.

### Rebuilding (2020–today)

The Giants moved into MetLife Stadium, shared with the Jets, in 2010. After a poor 2025, they hired John Harbaugh, Super Bowl winner with Baltimore, in January 2026. Jaxson Dart, Malik Nabers and Abdul Carter lead the young team.
