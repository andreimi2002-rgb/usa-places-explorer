### The Impact (1993–2011)

The Saputo family founded the Montreal Impact in 1992, and it played in lower leagues from 1993, winning the USL title in 2009. Saputo Stadium opened in 2008.

### MLS (2012–today)

Montréal joined MLS in 2012. Marco Di Vaio was its first star, and in 2015 the club reached the CONCACAF Champions League final, losing to Club América. Drogba joined that summer. In January 2021 the club was renamed Club de Foot Montréal, now CF Montréal. It has won the Canadian Championship several times, including 2013, 2014 and 2019.
