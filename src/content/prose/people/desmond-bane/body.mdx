### Memphis

Desmond Bane, from Richmond, Indiana, played four college seasons at TCU and was the 30th and last pick of the first round in 2020. He spent five seasons with the Memphis Grizzlies and grew from a late pick into one of the league's most reliable shooters: over the past five seasons he has averaged 20.2 points per game and made 40.4 per cent of his three-pointers.

### Orlando

Orlando added him in 2025 to bring outside shooting to a team built on defence, the weak spot of its game for more than a decade.
