### A new team (2002–2022)

Founded in 1999, the Texans began play in 2002 in a new stadium with a retractable roof. The receiver Andre Johnson and the defensive end J. J. Watt, three times Defensive Player of the Year, were their first stars.

### Ryans and Stroud (2023–today)

DeMeco Ryans, a former Texans linebacker, became coach in 2023, the year C. J. Stroud was drafted. In 2025 Houston went 12–5 after losing its first three games and won its first road playoff game, 30–6 in Pittsburgh.
