---
title: Why this team matters
---

Toronto FC was MLS's first Canadian club, joining in 2007. Its high point came in 2017, when it won the <Term id="supporters-shield">Supporters' Shield</Term>, the <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cup</Term> and the Canadian Championship, the first domestic treble in MLS history. Sebastian Giovinco, signed from Juventus, was the star of that era.
