---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Blue Jays are Major League Baseball's only Canadian team. Founded in 1977, they won back-to-back <Term id="world-series">World Series</Term> in 1992 and 1993, the second with Joe Carter's famous series-winning <Term id="home-run">home run</Term>. In 2025 they reached the World Series again and lost to the Dodgers in seven games.
