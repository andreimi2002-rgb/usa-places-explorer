---
title: Career in Orlando
---

Dwight Howard came to Orlando as the first overall pick of the 2004 <Term id="nba-draft">draft</Term> and spent eight seasons there. A powerful, athletic center, he became the league's best defender, named Defensive Player of the Year three seasons in a row from 2008–09.

In 2009 he led the Magic to their second NBA Finals, which they lost to the Lakers. In August 2012 he was moved to Los Angeles in a four-team <Term id="trade">trade</Term>. He was inducted into both the Naismith Hall of Fame and the Magic's Hall of Fame in 2025.
