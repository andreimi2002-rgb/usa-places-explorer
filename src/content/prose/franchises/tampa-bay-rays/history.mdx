### The Devil Rays (1998–2007)

The club began in 1998 at Tropicana Field in St. Petersburg and struggled for a decade.

### The Rays (2008–2024)

Renamed the Rays in 2008, the team reached the World Series that year with Evan Longoria and Carl Crawford. Kevin Cash, manager since 2015, took them back in 2020.

### Today (2025–today)

Hurricane Milton tore the roof of Tropicana Field in October 2024, so the Rays played 2025 at Steinbrenner Field in Tampa. Patrick Zalupski bought the club in 2025. Back home in 2026, the Rays won 98 games and the AL East and reached the World Series, lost to Milwaukee.
