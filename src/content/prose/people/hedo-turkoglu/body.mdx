---
title: Biography
---

Hedo Türkoğlu, born in Istanbul, came to the NBA with Sacramento in 2000 and joined Orlando in 2004. A tall forward who could handle the ball and make late shots, he was named Most Improved Player for 2007-08 and was one of the key players of the 2009 Finals team. After a year away he returned in 2010 and stayed until 2013.
