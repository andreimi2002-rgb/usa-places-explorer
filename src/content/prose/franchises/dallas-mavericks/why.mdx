---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Mavericks joined the NBA in 1980, and for most of their history they have been built around a European star. Dirk Nowitzki, a German forward, played all 21 of his seasons in Dallas and led the club to its only title in 2011. Luka Dončić, from Slovenia, followed him.

Today the Mavericks are rebuilding around Cooper Flagg, with a new coach, Dusty May, who won the NCAA title with Michigan in 2026.
