### Early years (1995–1998)

Toronto joined the NBA as an expansion team in 1995, with Damon Stoudamire as its first star.

### Vince Carter (1998–2005)

Vince Carter arrived in 1998 and was Rookie of the Year. The team moved into today's Scotiabank Arena in 1999. Tracy McGrady had begun his career in Toronto a year earlier.

### Bosh, DeRozan and Lowry (2003–2018)

Chris Bosh led the team in the 2000s, and DeMar DeRozan, drafted in 2009, and Kyle Lowry, who arrived in 2012, led the team in the 2010s.

### Kawhi and the title (2018–today)

In 2018 Toronto traded for Kawhi Leonard. With Lowry, Pascal Siakam, Fred VanVleet and Marc Gasol, the Raptors won the 2019 title and Leonard was <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>. He left for the Clippers and returned in 2026. Coached by Darko Rajaković, the 2026–27 team pairs him with Scottie Barnes, RJ Barrett and Immanuel Quickley.
