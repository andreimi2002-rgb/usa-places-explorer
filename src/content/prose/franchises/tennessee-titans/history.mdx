### The Houston Oilers (1960–1996)

The Oilers won the 1960 and 1961 AFL titles. Later stars included the running back Earl Campbell, MVP in 1979, and the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Warren Moon.

### Tennessee (1997–2024)

The team played in Memphis in 1997 and Nashville in 1998, and took the Titans name in 1999, with a new stadium. Under Jeff Fisher, Steve McNair and Eddie George took them to Super Bowl XXXIV. Derrick Henry later led the NFL in rushing twice.

### Today (2025–today)

The Titans drafted the quarterback Cam Ward first overall in 2025 but finished 3–14 again, and hired Robert Saleh in January 2026.
