---
title: NBA Draft
---

Every summer NBA teams take turns choosing the best young players, mostly from American colleges and international clubs. Players cannot simply sign with the team they like best at the start; the draft decides who gets them.

The order matters because it is built to help weaker teams: those with the worst records get the best chances at the top picks, decided by the draft lottery. That is why a single lucky draft, such as Orlando's in 1992 or 2004, can change a club for a decade. Teams can also trade their picks, sometimes years in advance.
