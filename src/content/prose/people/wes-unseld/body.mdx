---
title: Career
---

Baltimore drafted Wes Unseld second in 1968, and in his first season he was both <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> and Rookie of the Year. He played all thirteen seasons for the Bullets, won the 1978 title as <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>, entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 1988 and died on June 2, 2020.
