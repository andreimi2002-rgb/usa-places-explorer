### A record debut (2019–2023)

MLS approved St. Louis on August 20, 2019. The club's first season, 2023, was a surprise: coached by Bradley Carnell, with the Swiss goalkeeper Roman Bürki, it topped the Western Conference before losing in the first round of the <Term id="playoffs">playoffs</Term> to Sporting Kansas City. Bürki was Goalkeeper of the Year.

### Today (2024–today)

Carnell was dismissed in July 2024. In 2026 the club is coached by Yoann Damet.
