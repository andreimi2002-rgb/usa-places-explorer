---
title: About the city
---

Nashville is the capital of Tennessee, famous as Music City, the home of country music. The NFL's Titans have played here since 1998, first as the Tennessee Oilers. Nashville SC joined MLS in 2020 and plays in the largest soccer-specific stadium in the United States. The city is also home to the NHL's Predators. Nashville runs on Central Time, six hours behind London and eight behind Bucharest for most of the year.
