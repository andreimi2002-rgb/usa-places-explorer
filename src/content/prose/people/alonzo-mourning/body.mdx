---
title: Career
---

Alonzo Mourning was drafted second overall by Charlotte in 1992 and came to Miami in 1995, the same year as Pat Riley. A powerful center and shot-blocker, he was named Defensive Player of the Year twice, in 1999 and 2000.

After a spell with New Jersey he returned to Miami in 2005 and won his only title the following year, in 2006. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2014.
