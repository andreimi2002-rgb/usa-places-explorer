---
title: About the city
---

Miami, in the far south-east of Florida, has had an NBA team since 1988, when the Heat began play. The team has never moved or changed its name.

Miami runs on Eastern Time, the same as New York. For most of the year that puts it five hours behind London and seven behind Bucharest, so evening games start in the middle of the European night. The clock on this page does the maths for your own time zone.
