---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Phillies have played in Philadelphia under the same name since 1883, longer than any other American team has kept both name and city. Their history is long but not full of titles: they won their first <Term id="world-series">World Series</Term> only in 1980, with Mike Schmidt and Steve Carlton, and their second in 2008. Bryce Harper leads today's team.
