---
title: Biography
---

Darrell Armstrong took the long road to the NBA: he played at Fayetteville State, a small college, and only made his debut at almost 27, in April 1995. In Orlando, in 1998-99, he won two awards in the same season, Most Improved Player and Sixth Man of the Year. He is third on the club's career assists list and second in steals, and joined the Magic's Hall of Fame in 2020.
