---
title: Career
---

Houston drafted Hakeem Olajuwon first overall in 1984, and he played seventeen seasons for the Rockets. In 1994 he was league <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term>, Defensive Player of the Year and <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>, and he was Finals MVP again in 1995. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2008.
