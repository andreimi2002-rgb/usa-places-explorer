### Stars and a baseball stadium (2015–2020)

NYCFC entered MLS in 2015 together with Orlando City. Villa was the face of the team, its all-time top scorer and the league's MVP in 2016; Lampard and Pirlo joined him in the first seasons. Home games were played at Yankee Stadium in the Bronx.

### Champions (2021–today)

With the Argentine striker Valentín Castellanos, the Golden Boot winner, NYCFC won the 2021 MLS Cup on penalties against Portland. It added the Campeones Cup in 2022. Its own stadium, Etihad Park in Queens, is due to open in 2027.
