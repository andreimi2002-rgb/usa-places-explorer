---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Bengals were founded by Paul Brown, the coach who had given his name to the Cleveland Browns. They have reached three <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowls</Term> and lost all three: XVI and XXIII to San Francisco and LVI to the Los Angeles Rams. Since 2020 their <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> has been Joe Burrow.
