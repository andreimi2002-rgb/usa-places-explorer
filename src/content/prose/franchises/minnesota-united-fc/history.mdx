### Into MLS (2017–2018)

Minnesota became MLS's 22nd club in 2017, together with Atlanta United, and played its first two seasons at the University of Minnesota's football stadium. The Colombian Darwin Quintero led the 2018 team in goals and assists.

### Allianz Field (2019–today)

The club moved into its own stadium in Saint Paul in 2019 and reached the U.S. Open Cup final that year, losing 2–1 to Atlanta. The New Zealand defender Michael Boxall, a Loon since the first MLS season, is the captain.
