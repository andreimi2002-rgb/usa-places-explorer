---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Clippers began in 1970 as the Buffalo Braves, moved to San Diego in 1978 and to Los Angeles in 1984.

Steve Ballmer bought the club in 2014, and in August 2024 the Clippers opened their own privately funded arena, the Intuit Dome in Inglewood. They have not yet reached the <Term id="nba-finals">NBA Finals</Term>.
