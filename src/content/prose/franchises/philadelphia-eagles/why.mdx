---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Eagles are one of the NFL's oldest clubs, founded in 1933. They won NFL championships in 1948, 1949 and 1960 and two <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowls</Term>: LII over New England and LIX over Kansas City, with the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Jalen Hurts as MVP.
