---
title: Career
---

Boston drafted Paul Pierce tenth overall in 1998, and he spent fifteen seasons with the Celtics. In 2008, with Kevin Garnett and Ray Allen, he won the title against the Lakers and was named <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>.

He later played for Brooklyn, Washington and the Clippers, and entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2021.
