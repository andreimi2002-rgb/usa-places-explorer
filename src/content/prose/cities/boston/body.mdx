---
title: About the city
---

Boston has had the Red Sox since 1901, at Fenway Park since 1912, and the Celtics since the NBA's first season, 1946–47. Baseball's Braves, today in Atlanta, were founded here in 1871 and stayed until 1952. The NFL's Patriots began in Boston in 1960 and since 1971 have played in Foxborough, south-west of the city, where the Revolution, a founding MLS club, also play. Boston runs on Eastern Time, five hours behind London and seven behind Bucharest for most of the year.
