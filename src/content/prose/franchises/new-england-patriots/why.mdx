title: Why this team matters
---

The Patriots were the NFL's great dynasty of the 21st century. With the coach Bill Belichick and the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Tom Brady they won six <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowls</Term> between the 2001 and 2018 seasons, tied for the most with Pittsburgh. In February 2026 they reached Super Bowl LX, lost to Seattle.
