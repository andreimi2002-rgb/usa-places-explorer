---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Revolution are one of MLS's founding clubs and belong to the Kraft family, owners of the NFL's New England Patriots, whose stadium they share. They have reached five <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cup</Term> finals, in 2002, 2005, 2006, 2007 and 2014, and lost them all. Their best regular season brought the <Term id="supporters-shield">Supporters' Shield</Term> in 2021, with Carles Gil as MVP.
