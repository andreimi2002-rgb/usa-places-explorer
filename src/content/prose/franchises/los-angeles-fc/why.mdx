---
title: Why this team matters
---

LAFC is the younger of Los Angeles's two clubs, founded in 2014 and playing since 2018. It was good immediately: it won the <Term id="supporters-shield">Supporters' Shield</Term> in its second season, with Carlos Vela as MVP, and the <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cup</Term> in 2022. Its signings, from Gareth Bale and Giorgio Chiellini to Son Heung-min, have made it one of the league's best-known clubs.
