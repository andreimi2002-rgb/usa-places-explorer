---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Celtics are the most successful club in NBA history. Founded by Walter Brown in 1946, they have won 18 titles, one more than the Lakers, and eleven of them came in the thirteen seasons of Bill Russell's career.

They also matter for what they changed. Under Red Auerbach the Celtics built a dynasty, winning eight titles in a row from 1959 to 1966. When Auerbach stepped down as coach, Russell took over as <Term id="player-coach">player-coach</Term> and became the league's first Black head coach.
