### Vancouver (1995–2001)

The Grizzlies played their first six seasons in Vancouver, with Shareef Abdur-Rahim as their best player. The NBA approved the move to Memphis in July 2001, the league's first relocation since the Kings left Kansas City in 1985.

### The Gasol years (2001–2019)

Pau Gasol was the team's first star in Memphis. Later, Marc Gasol, Mike Conley, Zach Randolph and Tony Allen led the team, and Marc Gasol was Defensive Player of the Year in 2013. The team moved into FedExForum in 2004.

### Morant and Jackson (2018–2026)

Jaren Jackson Jr. arrived in 2018 and Ja Morant in 2019. Jackson was traded during the 2025–26 season, and Morant went to Portland before 2026–27.

### Boozer (2026–today)

Coached by Tuomas Iisalo, the 2026–27 Grizzlies build around rookie Cameron Boozer, center Zach Edey and guards Ty Jerome and Cedric Coward.
