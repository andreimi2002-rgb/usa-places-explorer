title: History by eras
---

### Beginnings (1970–2003)

The Cavaliers joined the NBA as an expansion team in 1970. In the late 1980s and early 1990s Mark Price and Brad Daugherty led the team. In 1994 they moved downtown into Gund Arena.

### LeBron, part one (2003–2010)

Cleveland drafted LeBron James first overall in 2003. Dan Gilbert bought the team in 2005, and in 2007 the Cavaliers reached their first Finals, where San Antonio swept them.

### LeBron, part two (2014–2018)

James returned from Miami in 2014 and, with Kyrie Irving and Kevin Love, reached four straight Finals against Golden State. In 2016 they won the title after trailing 3–1. James left for the Lakers in 2018.

### Mitchell and Mobley (2021–today)

The 2026–27 Cavaliers, coached by Kenny Atkinson, are built around Donovan Mitchell, James Harden, Evan Mobley and Jarrett Allen.
