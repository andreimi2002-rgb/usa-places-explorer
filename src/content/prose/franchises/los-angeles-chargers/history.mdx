### The AFL (1960–1969)

Founded in 1959 for the AFL, the Chargers moved to San Diego in 1961 and won the 1963 AFL title, with Lance Alworth at receiver.

### San Diego (1970–2016)

Dan Fouts led the passing teams of the late 1970s and early 1980s. The Chargers reached Super Bowl XXIX in the 1994 season. Junior Seau, LaDainian Tomlinson, Antonio Gates and Philip Rivers followed.

### Back in Los Angeles (2017–today)

The club returned to Los Angeles in 2017 and moved into SoFi Stadium, shared with the Rams, in 2020. Jim Harbaugh became coach in 2024; with the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Justin Herbert the Chargers went 11–6 in both of his first two seasons.
