---
title: Why this team matters
---

Portland is one of the places where soccer matters most in the United States. The Timbers name goes back to 1975, and the MLS club, founded in 2009, has played since 2011 in a downtown stadium backed by the Timbers Army. It won the <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cup</Term> in 2015 and reached the final again in 2018 and 2021.
