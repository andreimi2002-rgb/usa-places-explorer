---
title: City and fan culture
---

Chicago has had the Bulls since 1966, and the club has never moved or changed its name. The six titles of the 1990s all came within eight seasons, which is why the Jordan years still shape how the team is seen at home and abroad.

Chicago runs on Central Time, six hours behind London and eight behind Bucharest for most of the year, so a 7 PM tip-off in Chicago starts at 1 AM in London.
