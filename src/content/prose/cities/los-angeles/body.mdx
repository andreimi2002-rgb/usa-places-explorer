---
title: About the city
---

Los Angeles is home to two NBA teams: the Lakers, who arrived from Minneapolis in 1960, and the Clippers.

The city runs on Pacific Time, three hours behind New York. For most of the year that means eight hours behind London and ten behind Bucharest, so a Lakers game in the evening is an early-morning game in Europe. The clock on this page shows the gap for your own time zone.
