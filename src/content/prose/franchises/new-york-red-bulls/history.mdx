### The MetroStars (1996–2005)

The club played its first ten seasons as the MetroStars at Giants Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey, a huge NFL stadium.

### Red Bull (2006–today)

Red Bull bought the club in March 2006 and renamed it. In 2010 the team moved into its own soccer stadium in Harrison, New Jersey, and signed Thierry Henry. With Bradley Wright-Phillips, the club's record scorer, the Red Bulls won the Supporters' Shield in 2013, 2015 and 2018. They reached the MLS Cup final in 2008 and again in 2024, losing 2–1 to the LA Galaxy. Michael Bradley, the former Toronto captain, has coached the team since December 2025.
