---
title: About the city
---

Boston has had the Celtics since the league's first season, 1946–47; the club has never moved or changed its name. The NFL's Patriots began in Boston in 1960 and since 1971 have played in Foxborough, south-west of the city, where the Revolution, a founding MLS club, also play. Boston runs on Eastern Time, five hours behind London and seven behind Bucharest for most of the year.
