### Before Orlando

Pat Williams spent 56 years working in the NBA. He was general manager of the Chicago Bulls and the Atlanta Hawks and, from 1974 to 1986, of the Philadelphia 76ers, where he traded for Julius Erving and Moses Malone and built the team that won the 1983 title. He also drafted Charles Barkley.

### Founding the Magic

In 1986 he left Philadelphia to join the Orlando businessman Jimmy Hewitt in a bid to bring the NBA to Central Florida. The league awarded the city a franchise on April 22, 1987, and the team began play in 1989. As the first general manager, until 1996, Williams drafted Shaquille O'Neal and traded for Penny Hardaway, the pair that took Orlando to the 1995 Finals. He stayed with the club as a senior executive until he retired in 2019.

### Legacy

Williams received the Naismith Hall of Fame's John W. Bunn Lifetime Achievement Award in 2012 and was part of the first class of the Magic's Hall of Fame in 2014. He died on July 17, 2024, at 84. NBA commissioner Adam Silver said: "There is no Orlando Magic without Pat Williams."
