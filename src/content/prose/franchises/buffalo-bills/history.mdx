### The AFL (1960–1969)

Founded in 1959 as a charter member of the AFL, Buffalo won the league's championship in 1964 and 1965, and joined the NFL in the 1970 merger.

### Four Super Bowls (1986–1997)

Jim Kelly arrived in 1986, the year Marv Levy became coach. With Thurman Thomas, Andre Reed and Bruce Smith, the Bills reached Super Bowls XXV to XXVIII and lost them all, the first by one point.

### Josh Allen (2018–today)

The Bills drafted Josh Allen in 2018. Under Sean McDermott, coach from 2017 to 2025, they reached the playoffs seven times in eight seasons. Joe Brady became coach in 2026, the year the team moved into its new stadium.
