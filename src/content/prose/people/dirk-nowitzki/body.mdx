---
title: Career
---

Milwaukee drafted Dirk Nowitzki ninth in 1998, and he joined Dallas the same year. He played all 21 seasons of his career with the Mavericks, was league <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> in 2007 and won the 2011 title as <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2023.
