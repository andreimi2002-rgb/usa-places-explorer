---
title: About the city
---

Philadelphia has had the Phillies since 1883 and the Eagles since 1933; the 76ers arrived from Syracuse in 1963, and the NBA's Warriors played here from 1946 to 1962. The Union joined MLS in 2010 and play in Chester, south-west of the city. Philadelphia runs on Eastern Time, five hours behind London and seven behind Bucharest for most of the year.
