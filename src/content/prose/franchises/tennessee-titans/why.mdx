---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Titans began as the Houston Oilers, a charter member of the AFL, who won its first two titles in 1960 and 1961. They moved to Tennessee in 1997 and became the Titans in 1999. That season they reached <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowl</Term> XXXIV and lost it on the last play, stopped one yard short of the tying touchdown.
