### Baltimore (1953–1983)

The Colts began in Baltimore in 1953. Under Weeb Ewbank and then Don Shula, with Unitas, Raymond Berry and Lenny Moore, they won the NFL championship in 1958, 1959 and 1968. They lost Super Bowl III to the Jets and won Super Bowl V.

### Indianapolis and Manning (1984–2011)

The owner Robert Irsay moved the team to Indianapolis in 1984. Peyton Manning, drafted in 1998, won four MVP awards here and Super Bowl XLI; the Colts also reached Super Bowl XLIV. The team moved into Lucas Oil Stadium in 2008.

### Today (2012–today)

Shane Steichen has coached the team since 2023. The owner Jim Irsay died in 2025, and his daughter Carlie Irsay-Gordon became principal owner.
