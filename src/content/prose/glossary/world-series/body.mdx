---
title: World Series
---

The World Series is the final of Major League Baseball: the champions of the American League and the National League meet in a best-of-seven series in October, sometimes running into November. It has been played every year since 1903 except 1904, when the National League champions refused to play, and 1994, when a players' strike cancelled it. The New York Yankees have won it 27 times, far more than anyone else.
