---
title: Designated Player
---

MLS has a salary cap, which on its own would keep world stars out of the league. The Designated Player rule is the way around it: only part of a Designated Player's wages counts against the cap, and the club pays the rest.

The rule was created in 2007 so that the LA Galaxy could sign David Beckham. Today each club may name up to three, and they are usually its best-known players: Lionel Messi at Inter Miami or Son Heung-min at LAFC.
