---
title: How the NFL works
---

The National Football League has 32 teams, split into two conferences, the AFC and the NFC, each with four divisions of four. The regular season runs from September to early January, with 17 games per team, mostly on Sundays; the best seven teams of each conference then play knockout <Term id="playoffs">playoffs</Term>, ending with the <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowl</Term> in February.

The league was founded in 1920. Before the Super Bowl era it crowned its own champions, and from 1960 it competed with the American Football League until the <Term id="afl-nfl-merger">AFL–NFL merger</Term> of 1970. As in other American leagues there is no relegation; spending is limited by a <Term id="salary-cap">salary cap</Term>, and the weakest teams pick first in the draft.

This encyclopedia counts Super Bowls, from Super Bowl I (1966 season) to Super Bowl LX (2025 season). The New England Patriots and Pittsburgh Steelers have won the most, six each.
