### From Berlin

Franz Wagner was born in Berlin and played two college seasons at Michigan before Orlando chose him eighth overall in the 2021 <Term id="nba-draft">draft</Term>. He was named to the All-Rookie team in his first season.

### A versatile forward

Tall for a wing at 6 feet 10 inches (2.08 m), Wagner handles the ball and creates for others as well as scoring, and he became, with Paolo Banchero, the second pillar of the young Magic. Injuries limited him to 34 games in 2025-26, the season Orlando played a regular-season game in his home city, beating Memphis in Berlin.
