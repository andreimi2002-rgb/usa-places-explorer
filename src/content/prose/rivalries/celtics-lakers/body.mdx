---
title: Why this rivalry matters
---

Boston and Los Angeles are the two most successful clubs in NBA history, with 18 and 17 titles, and they have met in the <Term id="nba-finals">NBA Finals</Term> twelve times. Boston has won nine of those series and the Lakers three.

### The 1960s: Boston's decade

The first Finals meeting came in 1959, when Boston swept the Minneapolis Lakers. After the Lakers moved west, the teams met again in the Finals in 1962, 1963, 1965, 1966, 1968 and 1969, and Boston won every time. Bill Russell's Celtics beat a Lakers team led by Jerry West and Elgin Baylor again and again.

### The 1980s: Magic and Bird

The rivalry came back in the 1980s with Larry Bird's Celtics against Magic Johnson's Lakers. Boston won the 1984 Finals in seven games, and the Lakers won in 1985 and 1987.

### 2008 and 2010

The teams met twice more in the Kobe Bryant years. Boston, with Paul Pierce, Kevin Garnett and Ray Allen, won in 2008; the Lakers won the rematch in 2010, in seven games.

### Why it matters today

Every new title for either club changes the count at the top of the league. Boston's 18th, in 2024, put the Celtics back in front by one.
