---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Nuggets began in the ABA, a rival league, in 1967, and joined the NBA in 1976. For decades they never won the title, until Nikola Jokić, a second-round pick in 2014, became a three-time <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> and led them to their first title in 2023.

For a European reader, Jokić is the story: a Serbian center picked 41st, who became a three-time MVP.
