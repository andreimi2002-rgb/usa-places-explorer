### Beckham's project (2018–2022)

MLS approved the club on January 29, 2018, with Beckham and the businessmen Jorge and José Mas as owners. It began play in 2020, at a temporary stadium in Fort Lauderdale, north of Miami.

### The Messi years (2023–today)

Messi signed on July 15, 2023, and was soon joined by his former Barcelona team-mates Sergio Busquets, Jordi Alba and Luis Suárez. Inter Miami won the Leagues Cup in 2023, the Supporters' Shield in 2024 and, in 2025, its first MLS Cup, beating the Vancouver Whitecaps 3–1 in the final. In April 2026 the club moved into its permanent home, Nu Stadium in Miami.
