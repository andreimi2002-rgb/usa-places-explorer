title: Why this team matters
---

The Orlando Magic are proof that a brand-new team can matter quickly. Orlando joined the NBA as an <Term id="expansion-team">expansion team</Term> in 1989, a city better known for theme parks than basketball, and reached the <Term id="nba-finals">NBA Finals</Term> in only its sixth season.

The club's story is shaped by three moments of luck in the <Term id="nba-draft">draft</Term>: Shaquille O'Neal in 1992, Dwight Howard in 2004 and Paolo Banchero in 2022, each taken first overall. It has played in the Finals twice, in 1995 and 2009, and is still waiting for its first title, which gives every good Magic season a sense of unfinished business.
