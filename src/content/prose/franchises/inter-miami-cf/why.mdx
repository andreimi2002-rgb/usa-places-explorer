---
title: Why this team matters
---

Inter Miami is David Beckham's club: the former LA Galaxy star is one of its founding owners. It began play in 2020.

Its story changed in July 2023, when Lionel Messi signed. Within weeks Inter Miami won its first trophy, the Leagues Cup; it won the <Term id="supporters-shield">Supporters' Shield</Term> in 2024 and the <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cup</Term> in 2025, and Messi was named MVP twice.
