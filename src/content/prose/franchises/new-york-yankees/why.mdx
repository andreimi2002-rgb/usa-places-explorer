---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Yankees are the most successful club in American sport, with 27 <Term id="world-series">World Series</Term>, far more than any other team. Babe Ruth, bought from Boston in 1920, made them famous; Lou Gehrig, Joe DiMaggio, Mickey Mantle, Yogi Berra and Derek Jeter followed. Today their captain Aaron Judge holds the American League record of 62 <Term id="home-run">home runs</Term> in a season.
