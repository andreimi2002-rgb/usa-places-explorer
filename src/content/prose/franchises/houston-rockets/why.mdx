---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Rockets began in San Diego in 1967 and moved to Houston in 1971. Their great era came in the mid-1990s, when Hakeem Olajuwon led them to back-to-back titles in 1994 and 1995, both times as <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>.

Houston has also been home to some of the league's best-known names: Moses Malone, Yao Ming, Tracy McGrady, James Harden and, since 2025, Kevin Durant.
