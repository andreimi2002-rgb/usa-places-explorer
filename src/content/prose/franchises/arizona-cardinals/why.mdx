---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Cardinals are the oldest club in American professional football: founded in Chicago in 1898 and a charter member of the NFL in 1920. They won the NFL championship in 1925 and 1947, moved to St. Louis in 1960 and to Arizona in 1988. Their only <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowl</Term>, XLIII, was lost to Pittsburgh in the last minute.
