---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Chicago Fire were founded on October 8, 1997, the 126th anniversary of the Great Chicago Fire, and won the <Term id="mls-cup">MLS Cup</Term> and the U.S. Open Cup in their very first season. That team was built by coach Bob Bradley around the Polish playmaker Piotr Nowak. Later the club brought in Cuauhtémoc Blanco and Bastian Schweinsteiger.
