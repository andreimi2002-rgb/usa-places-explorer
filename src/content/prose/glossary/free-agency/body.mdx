---
title: Free agency
---

When a player's contract runs out, he can become a free agent and negotiate with any team, a little like a footballer leaving at the end of his contract. NBA free agency opens each summer at a set date.

There are two kinds. An unrestricted free agent can sign wherever he likes. A restricted free agent can receive offers from other teams, but his previous team has the right to match the offer and keep him. A well-known example is Shaquille O'Neal, who left Orlando for the Lakers as a free agent in 1996.

NFL free agency opens each March, at the start of the league year. MLS has had a limited free agency, for experienced players only, since 2015.
