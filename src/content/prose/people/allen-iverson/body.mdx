---
title: Career
---

Philadelphia drafted Allen Iverson first overall in 1996, and he was Rookie of the Year. He led the league in scoring four times and was <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> in 2001, when he took the 76ers to the Finals.

After spells with Denver, Detroit and Memphis he returned to Philadelphia in 2009–10. He entered the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 2016.
