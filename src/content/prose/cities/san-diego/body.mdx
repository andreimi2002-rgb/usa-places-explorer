---
title: About the city
---

San Diego had the NFL's Chargers from 1961 to 2016, before they returned to Los Angeles. In the NBA it had the Rockets from 1967 to 1971 and the Clippers from 1978 to 1984. San Diego FC joined MLS in 2025. San Diego runs on Pacific Time, eight hours behind London and ten behind Bucharest for most of the year.
