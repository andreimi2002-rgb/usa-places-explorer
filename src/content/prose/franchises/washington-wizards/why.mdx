---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Wizards were admitted in 1961 as the Chicago Packers, moved to Baltimore as the Bullets in 1963 and to the Washington area in 1973. As the Bullets they reached the Finals four times in the 1970s and won the 1978 title, with Wes Unseld as <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>.

Michael Jordan finished his career here, and in 2026 the team drafted AJ Dybantsa first overall.
