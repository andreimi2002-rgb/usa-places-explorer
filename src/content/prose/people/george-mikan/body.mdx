---
title: Career
---

George Mikan was the center of the Minneapolis Lakers in their first great years. He won the BAA title in 1949 and NBA titles in 1950, 1952, 1953 and 1954.

He briefly coached the Lakers in 1957–58. He was elected to the Naismith <Term id="hall-of-fame">Hall of Fame</Term> in 1959 and died on June 1, 2005.
