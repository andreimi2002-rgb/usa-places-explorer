### The USL years (2016–2018)

Founded in 2015, the club played in the USL from 2016 at the University of Cincinnati's Nippert Stadium and won the USL regular season in 2018, the year MLS awarded it a place.

### MLS (2019–today)

Cincinnati's first MLS match was a 4–1 loss in Seattle in March 2019, and its first seasons were hard. Things changed after TQL Stadium opened in May 2021 and Pat Noonan became coach that December: in 2023 the club won the Supporters' Shield on September 30, and Acosta was named MVP.
