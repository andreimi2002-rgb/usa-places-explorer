---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Grizzlies joined the NBA in 1995 in Vancouver, Canada, and moved to Memphis in 2001. Marc Gasol, Defensive Player of the Year in 2013, was at the center of their teams of the 2010s.

In 2026 they started again: Ja Morant was traded to Portland, and the team now builds around Cameron Boozer, the third pick of the 2026 draft.
