---
title: MVP
---

MVP stands for Most Valuable Player. In the NBA it is the main individual award of the regular season, roughly what the Ballon d'Or is in football, but for a single league.

It is separate from the <Term id="finals-mvp">Finals MVP</Term>, which is given only for the championship series.

The NFL also names an MVP each season, voted by media members for the Associated Press; it usually goes to a <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term>, such as Matthew Stafford in 2025. In MLS the award is called the Landon Donovan MVP.
