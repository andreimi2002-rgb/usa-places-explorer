---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Texans are the NFL's youngest team. Houston lost the Oilers to Tennessee in 1997, and the league gave the city a new club, which began play in 2002. They have not yet reached a <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowl</Term>, but with the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> C. J. Stroud and the coach DeMeco Ryans they have become a regular playoff team.
