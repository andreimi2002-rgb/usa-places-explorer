---
title: Why this team matters
---

The Colts have won a <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowl</Term> in each of their two cities. In Baltimore, the <Term id="quarterback">quarterback</Term> Johnny Unitas won the 1958 NFL championship game, often called "the Greatest Game Ever Played", and the team won Super Bowl V. In Indianapolis, Peyton Manning won Super Bowl XLI under Tony Dungy, the first Black head coach to win it.
