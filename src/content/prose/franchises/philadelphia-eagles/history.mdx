### The early titles (1933–1960)

Founded on July 8, 1933, the Eagles won back-to-back NFL titles in 1948 and 1949 under coach Greasy Neale and a third in 1960, with Chuck Bednarik playing both ways.

### Close calls (1961–2016)

The Eagles reached Super Bowl XV, lost to the Raiders, and under Andy Reid, coach from 1999 to 2012, with Donovan McNabb at quarterback, Super Bowl XXXIX, lost to New England. Jeffrey Lurie bought the club in 1994, and it moved into Lincoln Financial Field in 2003.

### Champions (2017–today)

The Eagles won Super Bowl LII, 41–33 over New England. Under Nick Sirianni they lost LVII to Kansas City, then won LIX against the same opponent, 40–22.
