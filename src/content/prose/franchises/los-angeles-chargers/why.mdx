title: Why this team matters
---

The Chargers spent their first season, 1960, in Los Angeles, then 56 years in San Diego, and came back to Los Angeles in 2017. In San Diego the "Air Coryell" passing offence of Dan Fouts changed the game, and LaDainian Tomlinson was <Term id="mvp">MVP</Term> in 2006. They have reached one <Term id="super-bowl">Super Bowl</Term>, XXIX, lost to San Francisco.
